## Introduction

Gérard Genette (1930–2018) is the leading figure of French *structuralist poetics*, and his work can be summed up in a single *methodological principle*: **first take apart relations that ordinary literary experience lumps together, then explain how they combine.** When an event happens and when it is told are two different things; who speaks and whose experience limits what we can know are two different things; whether a text counts as literature and whether it is good are two different things again. Most of the confusions in everyday talk about fiction, such as "the first-person point of view" or "an omniscient narrator," come from treating several such relations as one.

> 热拉尔·热奈特（Gérard Genette，1930–2018）是法国*结构主义诗学*的代表人物，他的工作可以概括为一条*方法原则*：**先把文学经验中惯常混在一起的关系拆开，再说明它们如何组合。** 一件事何时发生与它何时被讲述，是两回事；谁在讲述与谁的经验限定了我们能知道什么，是两回事；一个文本是否被当作文学与它是否优秀，又是两回事。日常谈论小说时的许多混乱，比如“第一人称视角”、“全知叙述者”之类的说法，都来自把几种这样的关系当成一种。

> - *structuralist poetics*：结构主义诗学。以语言学为模型，研究文学作品得以成立的一般规则和结构，而不是解释某部作品的具体意义。
> - *methodological principle*：方法原则。
> - *lump together*：把……混为一谈；笼统归并。
> - *omniscient narrator* \[ɒmˈnɪsiənt\]：全知叙述者。传统小说理论用语，热奈特认为它把“谁在讲”和“信息受何种限制”两个问题混在了一起。

What is the point of taking them apart? Imagine a daughter who discovers an old letter left by her mother. The same set of events can be written in three versions. If the daughter recalls the discovery many years later, the reader feels *regret*, because the teller already knows how things ended. If the mother records the day in her diary as she writes the letter, the reader feels uncertainty, because the diarist does not yet know what will happen. If a narrator who takes no part in the events stays close to the daughter's consciousness and follows her feelings moment by moment, the reader feels *immediacy*. **The events are nearly identical, yet the effects differ sharply, and only by separating "the events themselves" from "how they are told" can we explain the difference.**

> 拆开这些关系有什么用？设想一位女儿发现了母亲留下的一封旧信。同一组事件可以写成三个版本。如果由女儿多年后回忆当年的发现，读者感到的是*追悔*，因为讲述者已经知道结局。如果由母亲在写信当天记下日记，读者感到的是未知，因为写日记的人还不知道以后会怎样。如果由一个不参与事件的叙述者贴近女儿的意识、按她当下的感受来写，读者感到的是*临场感*。**事件几乎相同，效果却截然不同；只有把“事件本身”与“事件如何被讲出来”分开，才能解释这种差异。**

> - *regret* \[rɪˈɡret\]：n./v. 追悔；遗憾。
> - *immediacy* \[ɪˈmiːdiəsi\]：n. 临场感；直接性；即时性。

Genette's research proceeds along this line in three widening circles. The innermost concerns the internal structure of telling: time, mode, voice, and the crossing of narrative levels (Sections I–V). The next concerns relations between one text and others: quotation, rewriting, genre, and the titles and prefaces that surround a book (Section VI). The outermost concerns language as form, the boundaries of literature, and the way works of art exist and are received (Sections VII–IX). Section X then runs the tools together on a single passage, and Section XI turns them into a procedure you can reuse. **Read the guide with one aim in mind: not to memorize labels, but to be able to say, for any passage, *what the text is doing, what effect that produces, and what it means*.**

> 热奈特的研究沿着这条线索由内向外展开为三个圈层。最内一层是讲述的内部结构：时间、语式、语态以及叙述层次的越界（第一至五节）。第二层是一个文本与其他文本的关系：引用、改写、体裁，以及围绕一本书的标题和序言（第六节）。最外一层是作为形式的语言、文学的边界，以及艺术作品如何存在、如何被接受（第七至九节）。第十节把这些工具合起来用于同一段文字，第十一节再把它们整理成可以反复使用的程序。**阅读本文时请记住一个目标：不是背下术语标签，而是面对任何一段文字，都能说清“*文本在做什么、产生了什么效果、意味着什么*”。**

> - *in widening circles*：由内向外逐层扩展。
> - *narrative level*：叙述层次。讲述行为在嵌套结构中所处的位置，详见第四节。
> - *what the text is doing, what effect that produces, and what it means*：文本在做什么、产生了什么效果、意味着什么。对应第十节所说的形式判断、机制判断与意义解释三个层次。

## I. The Basic Framework: Story, Narrative, Narrating

In *Narrative Discourse* (1972), Genette builds all narrative analysis on three objects and the relations among them. The **story** (*histoire*) is the set of events reconstructed from the text, together with their temporal, spatial, and causal connections. The **narrative** (*récit*) is the discourse or text the reader actually holds, that is, the events after they have been arranged, selected, and worded. The **narrating** (*narration*) is the act of telling that produces this narrative, including when it takes place, who performs it, and to whom it is addressed.

> 热奈特在《叙事话语》（1972）中，把一切叙事分析建立在三个对象及其相互关系之上。**故事**（histoire）是从文本中重建出来的事件，以及事件之间的时间、空间和因果联系。**叙事**（récit）是读者实际面对的话语或文本，即事件经过排列、取舍、措辞之后的样子。**叙述**（narration）是产生这个叙事的讲述活动，包括讲述发生在何时、由谁进行、讲给谁听。

> - *story (histoire)*：故事。从文本中推断、重建出来的事件序列及其联系，是“讲了什么”。
> - *narrative (récit)*：叙事。读者直接接触的叙事文本本身，是“怎样排列与措辞”。
> - *narrating (narration)*：叙述。产生叙事文本的讲述行为，是“谁、何时、对谁讲”。中文里“叙事”与“叙述”易混，本文中前者指文本，后者指讲述行为。
> - 引文出处：Genette, *Narrative Discourse: An Essay in Method*, trans. Jane E. Lewin (Cornell UP, 1980)；原文为 *Figures III*（Seuil, 1972）中的 “Discours du récit”。

**The three are not on an equal footing.** The reader has direct contact only with the narrative; both the story and the act of narrating are *inferred* from it. We are never first handed a "draft of the events" and then shown how the author rewrote it; we have only the text, and both the events and the situation of telling must be reconstructed from it. This *asymmetry* is the starting point of everything that follows.

> **三者的地位并不对称。** 读者直接接触的只有叙事文本，故事和叙述行为都是从文本中*推断*出来的。我们从来不是先拿到一份“事件底稿”，再去看作者怎样改写它；我们手里只有文本，事件和讲述情境都要从文本中重建。这种*不对称*是后面一切分析的起点。

> - *on an equal footing*：地位平等；处于同等地位。
> - *infer* \[ɪnˈfɜː(r)\]：v. 推断；推论。
> - *asymmetry* \[eɪˈsɪmətri\]：n. 不对称。

To give the abstract concepts a concrete object, this guide uses a single case of its own invention throughout, *The Unsent Letter* (not a real published work). The passages in each section are separate rewriting experiments on the same material and should not be assumed to form one consistent version. The basic events are these:

- **Summer 1998**: the mother writes a letter announcing that she intends to leave home; the father locks it in a drawer.
- **2008**: the mother dies. In the end she never left.
- **2020**: the father dies, and the daughter, Lin Lan, finds the letter.
- **2022**: Lin Lan and her younger brother quarrel over the letter.
- **2025**: Lin Lan tells her brother how she found the old letter.

> 为了让抽象概念有具体的对象，本文全程使用一个*教学自拟*的案例《未寄出的信》（并非实际出版作品）。各节的片段是对同一素材所作的不同改写实验，不假定它们属于同一个自洽的版本。基本事件如下：
>
> - **1998年夏**：母亲写下一封打算离家的信；父亲把信锁进抽屉。
> - **2008年**：母亲去世。她最终没有离开。
> - **2020年**：父亲去世，女儿林岚发现了这封信。
> - **2022年**：林岚与弟弟因这封信发生争执。
> - **2025年**：林岚向弟弟讲述发现旧信的经过。

> - *of its own invention*：自拟的；虚构编造的。
> - *rewriting experiment*：改写实验。用同一素材写出不同版本，以观察某一形式选择造成的差异。

The asymmetry yields three *disciplines* of analysis. **First, the story is not a fully determinable "true draft."** If a novel offers only conflicting testimony, the analyst may be unable to establish whether the father hid the letter or the mother herself decided not to send it; both possibilities should then be kept open rather than settled by the critic for the sake of a tidy timeline. **Second, the same past can be presented under different conditions of evidence.** A novel can begin with the discovery in 2020 and then return to 1998; or it can never show 1998 directly and let the characters guess at it from paper, handwriting, and memory. Both point to the same past, but what the reader has to go on is entirely different. **Third, the act of narrating has its own time, audience, and purpose.** Lin Lan writing her memoir in 2025 may be explaining a misunderstanding, asking forgiveness, or defending herself, and her choice of material will differ accordingly. Note also that the real author typing a novel at a computer and Lin Lan "writing her memoir" inside the novel are acts on two different levels and must be kept apart.

> 由这种不对称可以引出三条分析*纪律*。**第一，故事不是一份可以完全确定的“真实底稿”。** 如果小说只提供互相冲突的证词，分析者可能无法确认是父亲藏了信，还是母亲自己决定不寄；此时应保留两种可能，而不是为了画出一条整齐的时间线而替作品裁定真相。**第二，同一个过去可以在不同的证据条件下呈现。** 小说可以从2020年发现旧信写起，再转回1998年；也可以始终不直接写1998年，只让人物凭纸张、笔迹和记忆去推测。两者指向同一个过去，读者手里的证据却完全不同。**第三，叙述行为有自己的时间、听众和目的。** 2025年的林岚写回忆，可能是在解释误会、请求原谅，也可能是在为自己辩护，目的不同，选择的材料也就不同。还要注意：现实作者在电脑前写小说，与小说里林岚“写回忆”，是两个不同层次的行为，必须分开处理。

> - *discipline* \[ˈdɪsəplɪn\]：n. 纪律；规范；学科。此处指分析时必须遵守的规则。
> - *determinable* \[dɪˈtɜːmɪnəbl\]：adj. 可以确定的。
> - *conditions of evidence*：证据条件，即读者获得信息的途径与范围。
> - *to go on*：（作为判断的）依据。

Borrowing an *analogy* from the grammar of the verb, Genette divides the relations among story, narrative, and narrating into five categories, the first three of which belong together under "time." **Order** compares the sequence of events with their sequence in the text. **Duration** compares how long events last with how much text they receive. **Frequency** compares how many times an event happens with how many times it is told. **Mode** concerns how, and within what limits, information is supplied to the reader, that is, what the reader can know and through whom. **Voice** concerns the act of narrating itself: who speaks, when, on what level, and to whom. The first four mainly concern the relation between story and narrative; voice concerns the relation of the narrating act to both.

> 热奈特借用动词语法的*类比*，把故事、叙事、叙述之间的关系分为五个范畴，其中前三个同属“时间”。**顺序**比较事件发生的先后与文本排列的先后。**时距**比较事件持续多久与文本给了多少篇幅。**频率**比较事件发生几次与被讲述几次。**语式**涉及信息以什么方式、在什么范围内提供给读者，即读者能知道什么、通过谁知道。**语态**涉及讲述行为本身：谁在讲、何时讲、处在哪一层、讲给谁。前四项主要处理故事与叙事的关系，语态则处理叙述行为与二者的关系。

> - *analogy* \[əˈnælədʒi\]：n. 类比；比拟。
> - *order (ordre)*：顺序。故事顺序与文本顺序的关系。
> - *duration (durée)*：时距。故事时长与文本篇幅的关系，热奈特以“速度”来衡量。
> - *frequency (fréquence)*：频率。事件发生次数与讲述次数的关系。
> - *mode*：语式。信息的调节方式，包括距离与聚焦两个变量。
> - *voice (voix)*：语态。叙述行为本身的情境，包括叙述时间、叙述层次、叙述者与故事的关系。

These names are only borrowed by analogy: "time," "mode," and "voice" are **not** the grammatical tense, mood, and active or passive voice. To avoid confusion, this guide uses three fixed terms. **Story time** is when events actually happen. **Narrative time** is how events are arranged and how much space they occupy in the text. **Time of narrating** is the temporal position of the telling act, as in "Lin Lan, in 2025, is telling."

> 这些名称只是借来的类比：“时间”、“语式”、“语态”**并不等于**语法中的时态、语气和主动或被动语态。为避免混淆，本文统一使用三个术语：**故事时间**指事件实际发生的时间；**叙事时间**指事件在文本中的排列方式和所占篇幅；**叙述时间**指讲述行为所处的时间位置，例如“2025年的林岚正在讲”。

> - *story time*：故事时间。
> - *narrative time*：叙事时间，即文本中的排列与篇幅。
> - *time of narrating*：叙述时间，即讲述行为相对于事件的时间位置，详见第四节。

*Narrative Discourse* was originally a long essay in Genette's collection *Figures III*, and almost the whole of it takes Proust's *In Search of Lost Time* as its object. **It is therefore at once a general theory and a study of a single work**: its concepts arose from a concrete reading and were tested against an extraordinarily complex novel. Proust's interweaving of times and his shifts of narration constantly overflow the categories, and Genette himself repeatedly discusses exceptions and mixed forms; eleven years later, in *Narrative Discourse Revisited* (1983), he answered his critics and revised several concepts. **When learning the categories, pay as much attention to their edges and revisions as to the tidiest examples.**

> 《叙事话语》原是热奈特文集《辞格三集》中的一篇长文，几乎通篇以普鲁斯特的《追忆似水年华》为分析对象。**所以它既是一般理论，也是一部作品专论**：概念从具体阅读中产生，又在一部极其复杂的小说面前接受检验。普鲁斯特的时间交错和叙述变化常常越出分类的边界，热奈特本人也在书中反复讨论例外与混合形态；十一年后，他在《新叙事话语》（1983）中回应批评、修订了若干概念。**学习这些范畴时，应同样重视它们的边缘与修订，而不只记住最整齐的例子。**

> - *overflow* \[ˌəʊvəˈfləʊ\]：v. 溢出；超出（范围）。
> - *mixed form*：混合形态。
> - *Narrative Discourse Revisited*：《新叙事话语》（*Nouveau Discours du récit*, 1983；英译 1988），热奈特回应批评、修订聚焦等概念的续作。
> - 引文出处：Genette, *Narrative Discourse Revisited*, trans. Jane E. Lewin (Cornell UP, 1988)。

## II. Time: Order, Duration, Frequency

Each of the three items of temporal analysis answers one question about a piece of information: **where in the text does it appear** (order), **how much space does it receive** (duration), and **how many times is it told** (frequency). All three can act on the same event at once, so in analysis they must be described separately.

> 时间分析的三个项目，各自就一条信息回答一个问题：它**出现在文本的什么位置**（顺序），**占了多少篇幅**（时距），**被讲了几次**（频率）。三者可以同时作用于同一个事件，因此分析时必须分别描述。

> - *at once*：同时；立刻。此处取“同时”义。

**Order compares two timelines: the sequence in which events happen and the sequence in which the text tells them.** Call the writing of the letter A, its hiding B, the mother's death C, and the discovery D. The events occur as A → B → C → D; if the text tells them as D → A → B → C, it gives the result first (the daughter has found the letter) and then leads the reader back step by step to the causes. Genette calls every discordance between the two timelines an *anachrony*. To judge one, we need a point of reference: the main line the text is currently telling, which Genette calls the *first narrative* (*récit premier*). Relative to it there are two main kinds of anachrony. An *analepsis* returns, from some point on the main line, to tell what happened earlier: "Lin Lan unfolded the letter. Twenty-two years before, her mother had sat at this desk all night." A *prolepsis* tells in advance what has not yet happened relative to the main line: "She put the letter in her pocket, not yet knowing that two years later it would split brother and sister for good." **"Past" and "future" here are measured from the first narrative, not from the year the real reader lives in.**

> **顺序比较两条时间线：事件实际发生的先后，与文本讲述它们的先后。** 设写信为A、藏信为B、母亲去世为C、发现旧信为D。事件的发生顺序是 A → B → C → D；如果文本按 D → A → B → C 来讲，就是先给出结果（女儿发现了信），再引读者一步步回溯原因。两条时间线不一致之处，热奈特统称为*时间倒错*。要判断倒错，先要有一个参照点：文本当前正在讲述的那条主线，热奈特称之为*第一叙事*。相对于第一叙事，时间倒错主要有两种。*回叙*是主线讲到某一点后，返回去讲此前发生的事：“林岚展开信纸。二十二年前，母亲曾在这张桌前坐了整夜。”*预叙*是提前讲述相对于主线尚未发生的事：“她把信放进口袋，还不知道两年后它会使姐弟彻底决裂。”**这里的“过去”和“未来”都以第一叙事为准，与现实读者身处哪一年无关。**

> - *anachrony (anachronie)* \[əˈnækrəni\]：时间倒错。文本顺序与故事顺序之间的一切不一致。
> - *first narrative (récit premier)*：第一叙事。判断时间倒错时作为参照的那条主线。
> - *analepsis (analepse)* \[ˌænəˈlepsɪs\]：回叙，也译“倒叙”。在主线的某一点回头讲述此前的事件。
> - *prolepsis (prolepse)* \[prəʊˈlepsɪs\]：预叙。提前讲述相对于主线尚未发生的事件。
> - *for good*：永久地；彻底地。
> - 引文出处：Genette, *Narrative Discourse*, ch. 1 “Order”；另参 Scheffel, Weixler & Werner, “Time,” *The Living Handbook of Narratology* (2014)。

This lets us avoid three common misjudgments. **Telling something from the past is not necessarily an analepsis**: "In 2020 she went back to the old house" may simply be the starting point of the story, and only when a main line has been established and the text turns back does an analepsis occur. **A character talking about the future is not a prolepsis**: if a character says "I may leave tomorrow," that is something she says now, an event of the present; only when the narrative itself confirms a future fact is there a prolepsis. **Foreshadowing is not a prolepsis**: a train ticket on the desk can hint at a departure, but it does not narrate the future departure.

> 这样就能避开三类常见的误判。**讲到过去的事，不一定是回叙**：“2020年，她回到旧屋”可能只是故事的起点；只有当主线已经建立、文本又折返回去时，才构成回叙。**人物谈论未来，不是预叙**：人物说“我明天可能离开”，是她此刻说的一句话，属于当下发生的事件；只有叙事本身确认了未来发生的事实，才是预叙。**伏笔不是预叙**：桌上的车票可以暗示出走，但并没有讲述将来的出走。

> - *misjudgment* \[ˌmɪsˈdʒʌdʒmənt\]：n. 误判。
> - *foreshadowing*：伏笔；预示。只是暗示可能的结果，并不讲述未来的事件本身。

An anachrony can be measured on two scales. Its **reach** (*portée*) is how far it jumps away from the current moment; its **extent** (*amplitude*) is how much story time it covers once it has jumped. A return from 2020 to a single night in 1998 has a reach of about twenty-two years and an extent of one night. **A large reach combined with a small extent makes the long aftermath of a brief experience stand out.** Analepses can also be placed relative to the first narrative. Suppose a version whose main line covers 2020 to 2025. An *external analepsis* lies wholly before the main line begins (a return to 1998); an *internal analepsis* falls within the main line's range (a return from 2025 to 2021); a *mixed analepsis* starts before the main line and runs into it (from 1998 right through to 2021). Here "internal" and "external" refer only to the range of story time and have nothing to do with narrative levels or focalization, which happen to use the same words; do not confuse the three sets of terms. The range of the first narrative must also be identified from the work itself: if a novel opens with one line set in 2025 and then spends almost all its pages on the summer of 1998, then 1998 is the main line, and the whole novel cannot be counted as one gigantic analepsis.

> 时间倒错可以用两个尺度来测量。**跨度**是它跳离当前时点有多远；**幅度**是跳过去之后讲述覆盖了多长的故事时间。从2020年回到1998年的某一夜，跨度约二十二年，幅度只有一夜。**大跨度配小幅度，能让一段短暂经历的长久余波格外突出。** 回叙还可以按它与第一叙事的相对位置来定位。假设某个版本的主线覆盖2020至2025年：*外部回叙*整段落在主线开始之前（回到1998年）；*内部回叙*落在主线的时间范围之内（从2025年回到2021年）；*混合回叙*从主线开始之前讲起，一直延伸进主线范围（从1998年一直讲到2021年）。这里的“内部”、“外部”只以故事时间的范围为准，与后文的叙述层次、聚焦毫无关系，三套术语恰好用了同样的字眼，切勿混淆。第一叙事的范围也须从作品本身辨认：如果小说开篇只有一句写2025年，此后几乎全部篇幅都在写1998年的夏天，那么1998年才是主线，不能把整部小说算作一次巨大的回叙。

> - *reach (portée)*：跨度。时间倒错与当前时点之间的时间距离。
> - *extent (amplitude)*：幅度。时间倒错本身所覆盖的故事时长。
> - *aftermath* \[ˈɑːftəmæθ\]：n. 余波；后果。
> - *external / internal / mixed analepsis*：外部 / 内部 / 混合回叙。按回叙所涉时段与第一叙事时间范围的关系划分。

Genette also distinguishes analepses by function. A **completing analepsis** fills a gap that has not been told before; a **repeating analepsis** returns to an event already told, possibly changing its details, context, or meaning. Suppose that when the father's locking of the drawer is first narrated, Lin Lan assumes he is merely putting papers away, and only when the text returns to the same act is it revealed that he was locking up the mother's letter: that is a repeating analepsis, in which the same act acquires a new meaning. If the earlier text had never mentioned the drawer, the later revelation would be a completing analepsis. **What order changes is the course by which the reader comes to know things.** If the hiding is told before the discovery, the reader knows the truth before the daughter does, and the tension comes from waiting for her to find out; if the discovery comes first and the hiding is gradually supplied, the reader investigates alongside her, and the effect leans toward inquiry and reinterpretation. These are effects that particular arrangements may produce, **not fixed formulas** such as "prolepsis always creates a sense of fate" or "flashback always creates suspense."

> 热奈特还按功能区分回叙。**补充性回叙**填补此前没有讲过的空缺；**重复性回叙**回到已经讲过的事件，可能改变其细节、语境或含义。假设第一次写父亲锁抽屉时，林岚以为他只是在收好文件，直到文本回到同一个动作，才揭示他锁进去的是母亲的信，这就是重复性回叙，同一个动作由此获得了新的意义。如果前文从未写过锁抽屉，后来的揭示就是补充性回叙。**顺序改变的，是读者认识事情的进程。** 先写藏信、再写发现，读者比女儿更早知道真相，紧张感来自等待她发现；先写发现、再逐步补出藏信，读者与她一同追查，效果偏向调查与重新理解。这些都是具体安排可能产生的效果，**而不是固定公式**，比如“预叙必然制造宿命感”、“倒叙必然制造悬念”。

> - *completing analepsis*：补充性回叙。补上先前遗漏的信息。
> - *repeating analepsis*：重复性回叙。回到已讲过的事件，往往改变其意义。
> - *lean toward*：倾向于；偏向。
> - *flashback*：倒叙；闪回。日常用语，大致对应回叙。

**Duration cannot be measured precisely**, because a year of story and a page of text are not the same kind of unit, and readers read at different speeds. Genette therefore describes it as **speed**: the ratio between the length of story time and the length of text. A narrative in which every passage corresponds to an equal stretch of story time, an *isochronous* narrative, is only a theoretical reference point; real narratives are always *anisochronous*, sometimes fast and sometimes slow. Changes of speed can be reduced to four basic movements. In a **pause**, the text continues while story time stands still, as when the narration halts and the narrator meditates at length on this family's understanding of silence. In a **scene**, the length of text roughly matches the duration of the story, as when Lin Lan's quarrel with her brother over the letter is rendered line by line. In a **summary**, a short stretch of text covers a long stretch of story: "For the next ten years she came home only at Spring Festival." In an **ellipsis**, a stretch of story time is skipped altogether without a word, as in a jump from 1998 straight to 2008.

> **时距无法精确测量**，因为故事里的一年与文本里的一页不是同一种单位，读者的阅读速度又各不相同。热奈特于是改用**速度**来描述它：故事时长与文本长度之比。每一段文字都对应等长故事时间的“*等时*”叙事，只是一个理论参照点；真实的叙事总是*非等时*的，时快时慢。速度的变化可以归结为四种基本运动。**停顿**：文本继续，故事时间却停住了，例如情节暂停，叙述者转而长篇议论这个家庭对沉默的理解。**场景**：文本篇幅与故事时长大致相当，例如逐句呈现林岚与弟弟围绕这封信的争吵。**概述**：用较短的篇幅交代较长的故事时间，例如“此后的十年，她只在春节回家。”**省略**：一段故事时间被完全跳过，文本中不着一字，例如从1998年直接跳到2008年。

> - *speed*：速度。故事时长与文本篇幅之比，热奈特用来替代无法测量的“时距”。
> - *isochronous / anisochronous* \[aɪˈsɒkrənəs\]：等时的 / 非等时的。
> - *pause*：停顿。故事时间静止而文本继续。
> - *scene (scène)*：场景。文本与故事时长大致相等，典型形态是对话。
> - *summary (sommaire)*：概述。以短篇幅压缩长时间。
> - *ellipsis (ellipse)* \[ɪˈlɪpsɪs\]：省略。故事时间被跳过，文本中没有对应部分。
> - 引文出处：Genette, *Narrative Discourse*, ch. 2 “Duration”。

Four cautions apply when using these concepts. **The "equality" of the scene is a convention**: reading a dialogue cannot take exactly as long as the characters' speaking did; dialogue is merely the conventional form closest to isochrony. **Summary and ellipsis differ** in that summary still tells you what happened in that time, while ellipsis says nothing; an ellipsis may be explicit ("ten years later") or implicit, inferable only from changes in a character's age or surroundings. **Description is not a pause**: "She felt along the desk, her fingertips crossing a crack until they reached the drawer handle" describes an object in detail, but the character's movement continues, so story time has not stopped; only when the text unfolds while story time is wholly still is there a pause. **Slow-down** (*scène ralentie*), in which a few seconds of hesitation fill several pages, is a possibility Genette discusses but does not list as a fifth basic movement; it is usable in analysis but should not be presented as a category on a par with the four.

> 使用这组概念时要注意四点。**场景的“相等”只是一种惯例**：读一段对话所需的时间不可能与人物说话的时间严格相同，对话只是最接近等时的惯例形态。**概述与省略的区别**在于，概述仍告诉你这段时间里发生了什么，省略则什么也不说；省略可以是明示的（“十年以后”），也可以是隐含的，只能凭人物年龄或环境的变化推知。**描写不等于停顿**：“她沿着桌面摸索，指尖越过一道裂缝，终于碰到抽屉把手”写了物体的细节，但人物的动作仍在推进，故事时间并没有停；只有文本展开而故事时间完全静止时，才是停顿。**减速**，即几秒钟的犹豫被写成几页，热奈特讨论过这种可能，却没有把它列为第五种基本运动；分析中可以使用，但不应说成与四种基本运动并列的原著类别。

> - *convention* \[kənˈvenʃn\]：n. 惯例；约定俗成的规则。
> - *explicit / implicit*：明示的 / 隐含的。
> - *slow-down (scène ralentie)*：减速。文本长于故事时长的情形。
> - *on a par with*：与……同等；与……并列。

Duration analysis should not stop at a vague remark like "the rhythm alternates between fast and slow." **What is more valuable is to compare how space is distributed.** If ten years of the mother-daughter relationship get one paragraph of summary while three minutes of opening the letter get several pages, the old life recedes into the background and the moment of discovering the truth becomes the experiential center of the book. Yet little space can also express a life that was repressed and could not be spoken, so it should not be equated directly with "unimportant"; one has to see whether the omitted stretch is repeatedly recalled later.

> 时距分析不应止于“节奏快慢交替”这类笼统说法。**更有价值的是比较篇幅如何分配。** 十年的母女关系只用一段概述，拆信的三分钟却写了好几页：旧日生活退为背景，发现真相的那一刻成了全书的经验中心。不过，篇幅少也可能是在表现某种被压抑、无法言说的生活，不能直接等同于“不重要”；要看被略去的部分是否在后文被反复召回。

> - *recede* \[rɪˈsiːd\]：v. 退后；后退；逐渐远去。
> - *experiential center*：经验中心。文本中被赋予最多感知与篇幅、构成阅读体验核心的部分。
> - *equate with*：等同于。

**Frequency compares how many times an event happens with how many times it is told.** Genette groups the possible relations into three. **Singulative** narration tells once what happened once, or tells three times what happened three times; the number of tellings matches the number of occurrences. **Repetitive** narration tells several times what happened once, as when the same opening of the letter is narrated by Lin Lan in Chapter 1 and retold by her brother in Chapter 4. **Iterative** narration tells once what happened many times: "Whenever there was a thunderstorm, her mother would check that drawer." **Before sorting cases into these three, first fix the unit by which "one event" is counted.** If Lin Lan unfolds and rereads the letter every day, many readings really do occur in the story, and narrating them one by one is singulative, not repetitive. Likewise, the image of "the letter" recurring throughout the book is not repetitive narration in the sense of frequency, because frequency counts events, not words or images.

> **频率比较事件发生的次数与被讲述的次数。** 热奈特把可能的关系归为三类。**单次叙述**：发生一次，讲一次；或发生三次，分别讲三次，讲述次数与发生次数一一对应。**重复叙述**：发生一次，讲好几次，例如同一次拆信，第一章由林岚讲述，第四章又由弟弟转述。**迭述**：发生许多次，只讲一次，例如“每逢雷雨，母亲都会检查那只抽屉。”**区分这三类之前，先要确定“一件事”的计数单位。** 如果林岚每天都把信展开重读，故事里本来就发生了许多次阅读，逐次讲述这些阅读属于单次叙述，而不是重复叙述。同理，“信”这个意象在书中反复出现，也不等于频率意义上的重复叙述，因为频率数的是事件，不是词语或意象。

> - *singulative (singulatif)* \[ˈsɪŋɡjələtɪv\]：单次叙述。讲述次数与发生次数相等。
> - *repetitive (répétitif)*：重复叙述。一次事件，多次讲述。
> - *iterative (itératif)* \[ˈɪtərətɪv\]：迭述。多次事件，一次讲述，常用于表现习惯和常态。
> - *unit of counting*：计数单位。判断频率前必须先确定什么算“同一件事”。
> - 引文出处：Genette, *Narrative Discourse*, ch. 3 “Frequency”。

**Iterative narration is especially suited to habits, sustained pressure, and patterns of life.** "All summer she wrote a letter every day and never sent one" compresses many days into a single shared state. If one of those days is then expanded into a detailed scene, it stands out from the routine and becomes a turning point. Genette also discusses the **pseudo-iterative**: the text claims "every Sunday was like this" and then gives a long conversation so singular in its details that it could not possibly have been repeated each week. This is Proust's signature device, using one concrete scene to stand for a whole stretch of life. When you meet such a passage, do not treat it as a factual error; see that it superimposes the typical and the particular.

> **迭述特别适合表现习惯、持续的压力和生活模式。** “整个夏天，她每天都写信，却从不寄出”把许多天压缩成一种共同的状态。如果其中某一天随后被展开为详细的场景，它就从惯常生活中凸显出来，成为一个转折点。热奈特还讨论了**伪迭述**：文本声称“每个星期天都是这样”，随后却写出一段细节极其独特、不可能每周原样重复的长对话。这是普鲁斯特的标志性手法，用一个具体场面代表一整段生活。读到这样的段落，不必把它当作事实错误，而应看到它把“典型”与“具体”叠在了一起。

> - *pseudo-iterative (pseudo-itératif)*：伪迭述。形式上声称是惯常发生的事，内容却具体到不可能反复发生。
> - *signature device*：标志性手法。
> - *superimpose* \[ˌsuːpərɪmˈpəʊz\]：v. 叠加；使重叠。

**The three work together.** The night the father hid the letter can be narrated twenty-two years later as an analepsis (order), presented at great length (duration), and told once by each of three characters (frequency). A shift in temporal position, a slowing of narrative speed, and a multiplication of tellings all happen at once; to say only "this uses a flashback" misses the other two mechanisms.

> **三者同时作用。** 父亲藏信的那个夜晚，可以在二十二年后以回叙方式出现（顺序），以很长的篇幅呈现（时距），并由三个人物各讲一遍（频率）。时间位置的移动、叙事速度的放慢和讲述次数的增加同时发生；如果只说“这里用了倒叙”，就漏掉了另外两种机制。

> - *mechanism* \[ˈmekənɪzəm\]：n. 机制；运作方式。

## III. Mode: Distance and Focalization

**Mode answers the question of what the reader can know and through what channel.** Under mode Genette discusses two variables. **Distance** is the degree to which the narrative intervenes in supplying information: the more the narrator summarizes, the greater the distance; the closer the text comes to presenting a character's words as spoken, the smaller it is. **Focalization** is the range within which information is restricted: whether it is unrestricted, or confined to what some character can know. The two affect each other but cannot replace each other. (In *Narrative Discourse* the chapter on mode comes before the chapter on voice, and this guide keeps that order.)

> **语式回答的问题是：读者能知道什么，又是通过怎样的渠道知道的。** 热奈特在语式之下讨论两个变量。**距离**是叙事在提供信息时介入的程度：叙述者概括得越多，距离越远；越接近原样呈现人物的话语，距离越近。**聚焦**是信息受到限定的范围：是不受限制，还是只限于某个人物能知道的东西。两者相互影响，却不能互相替代。（《叙事话语》中“语式”一章在“语态”之前，本文沿用这一顺序。）

> - *channel* \[ˈtʃænl\]：n. 渠道；途径。
> - *distance*：距离。叙事介入信息呈现的程度，介入越多距离越远。
> - *focalization (focalisation)* \[ˌfəʊkəlaɪˈzeɪʃn\]：聚焦。对叙事信息范围的限制，尤其指信息是否以及如何受限于某个人物的认知。
> - *intervene* \[ˌɪntəˈviːn\]：v. 介入；干预。
> - 引文出处：Genette, *Narrative Discourse*, ch. 4 “Mood”。

Distance is easiest to grasp through the question of how a character's words enter the text. Genette distinguishes three states and treats the interior monologue separately as an extreme form. In descending order of narratorial intervention:

- **Narratized speech** (*discours narrativisé*): the narrator summarizes the speaking as an event, and the actual wording is invisible. "She refused to hand over the letter." The reader knows only that a refusal took place.
- **Transposed speech** (*discours transposé*): the character's words are converted into the narrator's syntax, what is usually called *indirect speech*. "She said that she would not give him the letter the next day." The content is kept, but tone and wording are controlled by the narrator.
- **Free indirect style** (*style indirect libre*): a variant of transposed speech that drops the tag "she said" or "she thought," letting the character's tone surface directly within third-person narration. "She would never give him the letter tomorrow. Why should she? Who did he think he was?"
- **Reported speech** (*discours rapporté*): the character's words appear in an independent form as spoken, what is usually called *direct speech*. "'I will never give you the letter tomorrow,' she said."

> 距离最容易从“人物的话怎样进入文本”来理解。热奈特区分了三种状态，并把内心独白作为一种极端形态单独讨论。下面按叙述者介入程度由高到低排列：
>
> - **叙述化话语**：叙述者把人物说话当作一个事件来概括，具体措辞不可见。“她拒绝交出那封信。”读者只知道发生了一次拒绝。
> - **转换话语**：人物的话被转换进叙述者的句法之中，即通常所说的*间接引语*。“她说，她第二天不会把信交给他。”内容保留了，语气和措辞却由叙述者掌控。
> - **自由间接话语**：转换话语的一个变体，去掉“她说”、“她想”之类的引导语，让人物的语气直接浮现在第三人称叙述里。“她明天绝不会把信交给他。凭什么？他以为自己是谁？”
> - **转述话语**：人物的话以独立的形式原样出现，即通常所说的*直接引语*。“‘我明天绝不会把信交给你。’她说。”

> - *narratized speech (discours narrativisé)*：叙述化话语。把言语行为当作事件概述。
> - *transposed speech (discours transposé)*：转换话语。大致相当于间接引语。
> - *free indirect style (style indirect libre)*：自由间接话语。去掉引导语、在第三人称句法中保留人物语气的话语形式。
> - *reported speech (discours rapporté)*：转述话语。大致相当于直接引语，人物话语以原样出现。
> - *tag*：n.（引语的）引导语，如“她说”。
> - 引文出处：Genette, *Narrative Discourse*, ch. 4；另参 McHale, “Speech Representation,” *LHN* (2014)。

Three points matter for understanding these distinctions. **First, in Genette's scheme free indirect style belongs to transposed speech** rather than forming a fourth class, and research on the phenomenon predates him; he merely incorporated it into the analysis of distance. **Second, degree of intervention is not a scale of merit.** Direct speech seems to let a character "speak for herself," yet when to quote, how long, and with what surrounding commentary are still decided by the narrative, and behind a fictional dialogue there is no original recording to be faithful to. Genette concludes that the only thing a narrative can truly imitate verbatim is language itself; for events it can only create the illusion of seeing them first-hand. Hence *showing* and *telling* can describe differences of effect but should not be taken as an absolute boundary between the unmediated and the mediated. **Third, formal distance, emotional distance, and ethical identification are three separate things.** A very restrained summary can move us more than pages of direct speech, and long dialogue can make a character seem hollow and remote; how much the narrator intervenes, how close the reader feels, and whether the work endorses the character must each be argued separately.

> 理解这组区分有三个要点。**第一，在热奈特的分类中，自由间接话语属于转换话语**，而不是独立的第四类；对这一现象的研究也早于热奈特，他只是把它纳入距离分析的框架。**第二，介入程度不是优劣等级。** 直接引语看似让人物“自己说话”，但何时引用、引用多久、前后配什么说明，仍由叙事决定；虚构对话背后也没有一份必须忠实的原始录音。热奈特据此指出，叙事能够真正原样模仿的只有语言本身；对于事件，它只能制造“仿佛亲眼所见”的幻觉。所以，“*展示*”与“*讲述*”可以用来描述效果的差异，却不宜理解为“无中介”与“有中介”的绝对分界。**第三，形式上的距离、情感上的距离与伦理上的认同是三回事。** 一句极其克制的概述，可能比长篇直接引语更令人动容；大段对话也可能使人物显得空洞疏远。叙述者介入多少、读者感到多亲近、作品是否认同这个人物，三者需要分别论证。

> - *predate* \[ˌpriːˈdeɪt\]：v. 早于；先于。
> - *verbatim* \[vɜːˈbeɪtɪm\]：adv./adj. 逐字地（的）；一字不差地（的）。
> - *showing vs. telling*：展示与讲述。英美小说理论中的一对概念，前者指以场景、对话直接呈现，后者指由叙述者概括交代。
> - *unmediated* \[ʌnˈmiːdieɪtɪd\]：adj. 无中介的；直接的。
> - *ethical identification*：伦理上的认同，即作品或读者是否在价值上站在某人物一边。
> - 引文出处：Klauk & Köppe, “Telling vs. Showing,” *LHN* (2014)。

**Free indirect style must be judged in context.** "Why should she?" may be the character's reaction or the narrator's comment. In French, English, and similar languages, free indirect style is often accompanied by grammatical clues such as *backshifted tense*; Chinese lacks such markers, so one must attend more closely to word choice, tone, *deictics* (whose position "here" and "tomorrow" are measured from), and how the sentence links to the surrounding flow of consciousness. Note also that first-person inner speech without quotation marks ("Why should I give it to him?") is **free direct speech**, and should not be classed as free indirect style merely because the quotation marks are missing.

> **自由间接话语需要结合上下文判断。** “凭什么？”既可能是人物的反应，也可能是叙述者的评论。法语、英语等语言中，自由间接话语常伴有*时态后移*之类的语法线索；中文缺少这类标记，因此更要留意用词、语气、*指示语*（“这里”、“明天”以谁的位置为准），以及句子与前后意识活动的衔接。还要注意：没有引号的第一人称内心语言（“我凭什么交给他？”）是**自由直接话语**，不能因为省略了引号就归入自由间接话语。

> - *backshifted tense*：时态后移。转述时把现在时改为过去时等语法变化。
> - *deictics* \[ˈdaɪktɪks\]：n. 指示语，如“这里”、“现在”、“明天”，其所指取决于说话者的位置。
> - *free direct speech*：自由直接话语。不加引号、不加引导语的第一人称话语或思想。

**The value of free indirect style lies not only in "entering a character's mind."** It lets the narrator's syntactic frame contain the character's evaluative words, so that two voices can draw close and blend, or open an ironic distance. If the father thinks, "It was only an unsent letter; what was there to make a fuss about?", the text may simply be presenting his self-justification without endorsing it. Whether this amounts to *irony* depends on whether the words conflict with the facts, whether other characters are hurt by them, and whether the narrative leaves clues that refute them.

> **自由间接话语的价值，不只在于“深入人物内心”。** 它让叙述者的句法框架容纳人物的评价性用语，两种声音可以靠近、交融，也可以拉开反讽的距离。假如父亲心想“不过是一封没寄出的信，有什么值得大惊小怪的”，文本可能只是在呈现他的自我辩护，并没有替他背书。是否构成*反讽*，要看这些词语是否与事实冲突，其他人物是否因此受到伤害，叙事是否留下了反驳的线索。

> - *evaluative* \[ɪˈvæljuətɪv\]：adj. 评价性的。
> - *irony* \[ˈaɪrəni\]：n. 反讽。字面意义与实际意义、或人物认识与事实之间形成的反差。
> - *make a fuss about*：对……大惊小怪。
> - *refute* \[rɪˈfjuːt\]：v. 驳斥；证明……错误。

**Traditional terms such as "point of view" often conflate two questions: who speaks, and through whose experience we see.** In a first-person memoir the speaker is the older "I," but he may write only from what he saw and knew when young. Genette introduced "focalization" precisely to separate the two: who speaks belongs to voice (Section IV), through whom we perceive belongs to mode. In *Narrative Discourse Revisited* he further revised the question from "who sees?" to "**where is the focus of perception?**", stressing that focalization is first of all a **restriction** on narrative information, not a character "looking." A better question than "who sees?" is therefore: **by what rule does this passage decide which information to provide, and does some character's range of knowledge take part in that restriction?** "Seeing" here is also not merely visual; it includes knowing, feeling, guessing, and evaluating.

> **“视角”、“观点”之类的传统说法，常把两个问题混为一谈：谁在讲，以及透过谁的经验来看。** 一部第一人称回忆录里，讲话的是年老的“我”，但他可能只按年轻时的所见所知来写。热奈特提出“聚焦”，正是为了把两者拆开：谁在讲属于语态（第四节），透过谁来感知属于语式。在《新叙事话语》中，他又把问题从“谁在看”修订为“**感知的焦点在哪里**”，强调聚焦首先是对叙事信息的一种**限制**，而不是某个人物在“看”。所以，比“谁在看”更好的提问方式是：**这段叙事按什么规则决定提供哪些信息？某个人物的认知范围是否参与了这种限制？** 这里的“看”也不只是视觉，还包括知道、感觉、推测和评价。

> - *conflate* \[kənˈfleɪt\]：v. 混为一谈；合并。
> - *point of view*：视角；观点。传统小说理论用语，热奈特认为它混淆了语式与语态。
> - *restriction* \[rɪˈstrɪkʃn\]：n. 限制。热奈特后期强调聚焦的本质是对信息的限制。
> - 引文出处：Genette, *Narrative Discourse Revisited*；Niederhoff, “Focalization,” *LHN* (2013)。

Genette distinguishes three kinds of focalization. In **zero focalization**, information is not continuously restricted by any character's range of knowledge; the text can report what the parties do not know about one another, historical background, even future outcomes. This roughly corresponds to what is traditionally called "omniscient" narration. In **internal focalization**, information corresponds to one character's range of experience, and the reader knows only what that character can perceive, remember, or infer; this roughly corresponds to a "character's point of view." In **external focalization**, only characters' outward words and actions are presented, without entering anyone's mind; this roughly corresponds to "camera-like" objective narration. The common formula "the narrator knows more than, as much as, or less than the character" should be understood as a relation of **quantities of information** supplied by the text, not as an invitation to invent a mental state for a narrator who has not been given one.

> 热奈特区分三种聚焦。**零聚焦**：信息不受任何一个人物认知范围的持续限制，可以交代各方互不知晓的事、历史背景乃至未来的结果，大致相当于传统所说的“全知”叙事。**内部聚焦**：信息与某个人物的经验范围相对应，读者只知道这个人物能感知、记得或推想到的东西，大致相当于“人物视角”。**外部聚焦**：只呈现人物的言行等外在表现，不进入任何人的意识，大致相当于“摄像机式”的客观叙事。常见的说法“叙述者比人物知道得多、一样多或少”，应理解为文本所提供的**信息量关系**，而不是去替一个并未被具体塑造的叙述者编造心理状态。

> - *zero focalization*：零聚焦。信息不受某一人物认知的持续限制。
> - *internal focalization*：内部聚焦。信息限于某一人物的经验范围。
> - *external focalization*：外部聚焦。只呈现外在言行，不进入人物意识。
> - *camera-like*：摄像机式的。

Compare three ways of writing the same moment. First: "Lin Lan picked up the letter. She believed her father had never read it, not knowing that he had hidden it twenty-two years earlier; across the city, her brother was at that moment concealing something of his own about their mother." Second: "Lin Lan picked up the letter. The fold along the seal was very old. Had her father read it? She remembered him always saying this family had no secrets." Third: "Lin Lan picked up the letter, turned it over, and paused. She did not open it; she only put it in her coat pocket." The first reports both what Lin Lan does not know and her distant brother's secret, so information is not restricted by her: it is organized by zero focalization. The second gives only what Lin Lan sees, thinks, and remembers: internal focalization. The third gives only actions without her thoughts and can serve as a local example of external focalization. **But a single paragraph without psychology does not prove that a whole work is externally focalized**; one must observe whether a stable restriction holds across a larger range.

> 比较同一时刻的三种写法。第一种：“林岚拿起信。她以为父亲从未读过，却不知道他二十二年前已将它藏起；住在城另一端的弟弟，此时也正隐瞒着一件与母亲有关的事。”第二种：“林岚拿起信。封口的折痕太旧了。父亲读过吗？她记得他总说，这个家从来没有秘密。”第三种：“林岚拿起信，翻到背面，停了片刻。她没有拆开，只把它放进外套口袋。”第一段同时交代了林岚不知道的事和远处弟弟的秘密，信息不受她的限制，是零聚焦的组织方式。第二段只写林岚看到的、想到的、记得的，是内部聚焦。第三段只写动作，不写她在想什么，可以作为外部聚焦的局部示例。**但仅凭一段话没写心理，不能断定全篇采用外部聚焦**，必须观察更大范围内是否形成了稳定的限制。

> - *local example*：局部示例。只说明某一段的特征，不代表全篇。
> - *stable restriction*：稳定的限制。在较长篇幅内持续成立的信息规则。

Internal focalization can be organized in three ways. It is **fixed** when one character remains the center of experience throughout; **variable** when different parts move to different characters in turn (Chapter 1 follows Lin Lan discovering the letter, Chapter 2 follows her brother's life in another city); and **multiple** when the **same event** is presented repeatedly from several characters' positions (two chapters tell the same family quarrel from Lin Lan's side and from her brother's). **An important corollary follows: being able to enter several characters' minds does not prove zero focalization.** If each chapter is strictly confined to that chapter's character, the whole work may be using variable internal focalization. Conversely, a zero-focalized work need not be all-knowing in every paragraph and can stay close to one character for a while. In analysis, describe both **the local rule** and **the organization of the whole**, rather than erasing the variations for the sake of one label.

> 内部聚焦有三种组织方式。**固定内部聚焦**：始终以同一个人物为经验中心。**变换内部聚焦**：不同部分依次转到不同人物，例如第一章跟随林岚发现旧信，第二章跟随弟弟在另一座城市生活。**多重内部聚焦**：从几个人物的位置反复呈现**同一个事件**，例如两章分别从林岚和弟弟的角度讲同一次家庭争吵。**由此得出一个重要推论：能进入多个人物的内心，并不足以证明是零聚焦。** 如果每一章都严格限于该章人物的经验，全篇采用的可能是变换内部聚焦。反过来，零聚焦的作品也不必每段都无所不知，局部可以暂时贴近某个人物。分析时应同时描述**局部的规则**和**全篇的组织**，不要为了贴一个标签而抹掉其中的变化。

> - *fixed / variable / multiple internal focalization*：固定 / 变换 / 多重内部聚焦。
> - *corollary* \[kəˈrɒləri\]：n. 推论。
> - *for the sake of*：为了……的缘故。

**Once a work has established a rule of focalization, local breaches of it deserve attention.** Genette describes such breaches with two terms. **Paralepsis** gives more information than the established restriction allows: a novel that has stayed with Lin Lan's experience suddenly, for no reason, reports her brother's thoughts when he is alone. **Paralipsis** withholds information that the established rule ought to supply: a novel that has presented Lin Lan's thoughts in every quarrel in detail reveals only at the end that she knew all along the letter was forged. Both judgments must rest on **the rule the work has already established**. Internal focalization does not promise to report every second of a character's consciousness; not telling the reader something for a while does not automatically constitute paralipsis; a stable shift to another character is more likely a change in the mode of focalization than a breach. **These terms describe differences first; they do not prejudge writing faults.**

> **一部作品一旦建立了聚焦规则，局部的违反就值得注意。** 热奈特用两个术语描述这种违反。**信息增叙**：给出了超出既定限制的信息，例如小说一直贴着林岚的经验来写，却突然无来由地交代了弟弟独处时的想法。**信息省叙**：隐去了按既定规则本应提供的重要信息，例如小说在每次争吵中都细致呈现林岚的想法，却直到结尾才透露她早就知道那封信是伪造的。两种判断都必须以作品**已经建立的规则**为依据。内部聚焦并不承诺报告人物每一秒的全部意识；暂时没告诉读者某件事，不自动构成省叙；稳定地转向另一个人物，更可能是聚焦方式的变化，而非违规。**这些术语首先用于描述差异，而不是预先判定写作失误。**

> - *breach* \[briːtʃ\]：n./v. 违反；破坏；缺口。
> - *paralepsis (paralepse)* \[ˌpærəˈlepsɪs\]：信息增叙。提供了超出既定聚焦限制的信息。
> - *paralipsis (paralipse)* \[ˌpærəˈlɪpsɪs\]：信息省叙。略去了按既定聚焦规则本应提供的信息。
> - *prejudge* \[ˌpriːˈdʒʌdʒ\]：v. 预先判定；过早下结论。

Focalization remains a contested concept. The Dutch scholar Mieke Bal treats the *focalizer* as an active subject and argues for distinguishing the focalizer from the focalized object; Genette responded to this in *Narrative Discourse Revisited*, partly accepting it and partly holding back. **Focalization also overlaps with, but is not the same as, unreliable narration.** That Lin Lan does not know her father hid the letter is a **restriction of knowledge**, a matter of focalization. A narrator who repeatedly insists that "my mother never thought of leaving" while the text keeps supplying contrary evidence raises a different question: **whether the narrator's statements or judgments can be trusted.** The concept of the *unreliable narrator* comes from the American critic Wayne C. Booth's *The Rhetoric of Fiction* (1961) and was later developed in rhetorical and cognitive narratology; it is not Genette's own theory. Unreliability can occur at three levels: in **reporting facts** (the events themselves are told wrongly), in **interpretation** (the events are told rightly but their causes are misexplained), and in **evaluation** (facts and interpretation are sound, but the judgment of the conduct is questionable). Likewise, if third-person narration stays close to the father's consciousness and presents his contempt for his wife, first determine whose voice that contempt belongs to. A character's prejudice and a narrator's misstatement are different problems, and being able to enter someone's mind does not mean the work asks the reader to agree with him.

> 聚焦至今仍是一个有争议的概念。荷兰学者米克·巴尔把“*聚焦者*”理解为一个主动的主体，并主张区分“聚焦者”与“被聚焦的对象”；热奈特在《新叙事话语》中对此有所回应，也有所保留。**聚焦与不可靠叙述也有交集，但并非一回事。** 林岚不知道父亲藏过信，这是**认知限制**，属于聚焦问题。一个叙述者反复声称“母亲从无离开的念头”，而文本不断提供相反的证据，这引出的是另一个问题：**叙述者的陈述或判断是否可信。** “*不可靠叙述者*”这一概念出自美国批评家韦恩·布斯的《小说修辞学》（1961），后来在修辞叙事学和认知叙事学中继续发展，并非热奈特本人的理论。不可靠性可以出现在三个层面：**事实报告**（事件本身讲错了）、**解释**（事件讲对了，原因却解释错了）、**价值判断**（事实与解释都没有问题，对行为的评价却值得怀疑）。同理，如果第三人称叙述紧贴父亲的意识、呈现他对妻子的轻蔑，应先分辨这种轻蔑属于谁的声音。人物的偏见与叙述者的失实是两个不同的问题；能够进入一个人的意识，并不意味着作品要求读者赞同他。

> - *contested* \[kənˈtestɪd\]：adj. 有争议的。
> - *focalizer* \[ˈfəʊkəlaɪzə(r)\]：聚焦者。巴尔用来指承担感知与选择的那个主体。
> - *unreliable narrator*：不可靠叙述者。陈述、解释或判断与作品隐含的规范不符、令读者不能完全信任的叙述者。
> - *misstatement* \[ˌmɪsˈsteɪtmənt\]：n. 失实陈述；错误陈述。
> - 引文出处：Mieke Bal, *Narratology* (U of Toronto P, 1985)；Wayne C. Booth, *The Rhetoric of Fiction* (U of Chicago P, 1961)；Dan Shen, “Unreliability,” *LHN* (2013)。

## IV. Voice: Who Narrates, and from Where

**Voice concerns the act of narrating itself: when the telling takes place, on what level it stands, what relation the teller has to the story, and to whom it is addressed.** "Voice" (*voix*) here means the *instance* that produces the narration, not a loud voice or a friendly tone in the everyday sense. Before analysis, three roles must be separated: the **real author**, who actually wrote the work; the **narrator**, the voice in the text that does the telling; and the **characters**, who act in the story. A real author can set up a narrator who is selfish, mistaken about others, or deliberately lying; that a narrator or a character says something does not prove that the real author agrees with it.

> **语态关心的是讲述行为本身：讲述发生在什么时候、处在哪一层、讲述者与故事是什么关系、讲给谁听。** 这里的“声音”（voix）指发出叙述的那个*主体实例*，而不是“声音洪亮”、“语气亲切”这类日常意义。分析之前，先要把三个角色分开：**现实作者**，即真正写下这部作品的人；**叙述者**，即文本中承担讲述的那个声音；**人物**，即故事中行动的人。现实作者可以设置一个自私、误解他人甚至蓄意撒谎的叙述者；叙述者或人物说了一句话，不能证明现实作者赞同它。

> - *instance (instance narrative)* \[ˈɪnstəns\]：主体实例；叙述主体。指在文本中承担讲述功能的位置，而非现实中的人。
> - *real author*：现实作者。
> - *narrator* \[nəˈreɪtə(r)\]：叙述者。文本内部承担讲述的声音，不等同于现实作者。
> - 引文出处：Genette, *Narrative Discourse*, ch. 5 “Voice”；另参 Kaempfer & Zanghi, “La voix narrative,” Université de Genève (2003)。

**The telling act can occupy four temporal positions relative to the events it tells.** In **subsequent narrating** (*narration ultérieure*), the telling comes after the events, as when Lin Lan in 2025 tells of her discovery in 2020; most novels written in the past tense belong here. In **prior narrating** (*narration antérieure*), the telling comes before the events, as in prophecy or dream-omen narration about what has not yet happened. In **simultaneous narrating**, telling and events roughly coincide, as in present-tense "live report" narration. In **interpolated narrating** (*narration intercalée*), multiple tellings are inserted into the course of the events, as in a diary kept over time or an exchange of letters, where each time someone writes, things are still unfolding.

> **讲述行为相对于所讲的事件，可以处在四种时间位置上。** **事后叙述**：讲述发生在事件之后，例如2025年的林岚讲述2020年的发现；绝大多数用过去时写成的小说都属于这一类。**事前叙述**：讲述发生在事件之前，例如预言、梦兆式的叙述，讲的是尚未发生的事。**同时叙述**：讲述与事件大体同步，例如用现在时进行的“现场报道”式叙述。**插入叙述**：多次讲述穿插在事件进行的过程之中，例如持续写下的日记或往来的书信，每次写的时候，事情还在发展。

> - *subsequent narrating (narration ultérieure)*：事后叙述。
> - *prior narrating (narration antérieure)*：事前叙述。
> - *simultaneous narrating (narration simultanée)*：同时叙述。
> - *interpolated narrating (narration intercalée)* \[ɪnˈtɜːpəleɪtɪd\]：插入叙述，典型如日记体、书信体。
> - *omen* \[ˈəʊmən\]：n. 预兆；征兆。

This set of distinctions is the easiest to confuse with the anachronies of Section II, and the key is that **the objects of comparison differ**: anachrony compares textual order with story order, while time of narrating compares the telling act with the events. Three reminders follow. **Prior narrating is not prolepsis.** When Lin Lan in 2025, telling of her discovery in 2020, mentions in passing the quarrel of 2022, both events have already happened for her, so the whole remains subsequent narrating; only relative to the 2020 the text is currently telling is 2022 still in the future, so that sentence is a prolepsis. Prior narrating requires the telling act itself to precede the events. **A grammatical present tense is not simultaneous narrating.** Someone recalling things years later may write "I push open the door; there is no one in the room" for vividness; clues such as "now," "back then," and "only later did I learn" must be used to judge the situation of telling. **The local and the whole can differ.** Each diary entry is often a short-range subsequent narration of that day's events, while the set of entries taken together forms interpolated narrating.

> 这组区分最容易与第二节的时间倒错相混，关键在于**比较的对象不同**：时间倒错比较的是文本顺序与事件顺序，叙述时间比较的是讲述行为与事件。由此有三点提醒。**事前叙述不等于预叙。** 2025年的林岚讲述2020年的发现时，顺带提前说出2022年的争吵：对她而言两件事都已经发生，所以整体仍是事后叙述；只是相对于文本正在讲的2020年，2022年还在未来，所以这一句是预叙。事前叙述要求讲述行为本身发生在事件之前。**语法上的现在时不等于同时叙述。** 多年后回忆的人可以为了临场感写“我推开门，屋里没有人”，要结合“如今”、“当时”、“后来才知道”等线索判断讲述情境。**局部与整体可以不同。** 日记中的每一篇，往往是对当天事情的短距离事后叙述；整组日记合起来，则构成插入叙述。

> - *objects of comparison*：比较的对象。区分相近概念时，首先看它们各自在比较什么。
> - *in passing*：顺带地；附带一提。
> - *short-range*：短距离的。

**In a first-person text of subsequent narrating there are really two "I"s.** One is the Lin Lan of 2020 who is living through the events and may still trust her father; the other is the Lin Lan of 2025 who is telling and already knows more. The sentence "I did not yet know that the drawer held my mother's unsent letter" places past ignorance beneath present knowledge. This pair of concepts, the **narrating I** and the **experiencing I**, comes from German-language narrative studies (Spitzer, Stanzel, and others), and Genette likewise treats the tension between them in his analysis of Proust. **The relation between the two "I"s determines the nature of the memory.** If the text keeps strictly to the perceptions of the time, later knowledge is held back for a while and the reader is puzzled along with the earlier self; if the text keeps inserting later judgments, the memory takes on the color of explanation, self-examination, or even self-justification. First-person narration therefore does not supply a naturally unified point of view; it may be **an ongoing dialogue between a past self and a present self**.

> **在事后叙述的第一人称文本里，其实有两个“我”。** 一个是2020年那个正在经历事件、可能还信任父亲的林岚，另一个是2025年那个正在讲述、已经知道更多的林岚。“我那时还不知道，抽屉里藏着母亲未寄出的信”这句话，就把过去的无知置于现在的知识之下。这一对概念，即**叙述之我**与**经历之我**，最早来自德语叙事研究（施皮策、斯坦策尔等人），热奈特在分析普鲁斯特时同样处理了两者之间的张力。**两个“我”的关系，决定了回忆的性质。** 文本若严格贴近当时的感知，后来的知识就被暂时压住，读者与当年的“我”一同困惑；文本若不断插入后来的判断，回忆就带上解释、反省乃至自我辩护的色彩。所以，第一人称叙述提供的并不是一个天然统一的视点，而可能是**过去与现在两个自我之间持续的对话**。

> - *narrating I / experiencing I*：叙述之我 / 经历之我。回顾性第一人称叙事中，讲述时的“我”与当时经历事件的“我”。
> - *hold back*：抑制；暂时压住。
> - *self-justification*：自我辩护。
> - 引文出处：F. K. Stanzel, *A Theory of Narrative*, trans. Charlotte Goedsche (Cambridge UP, 1984)。

**Characters in a story can also tell stories, and in the stories they tell someone may tell another.** *Narrative levels* describe the position of a telling act within this nesting. Genette's definition is that any event a narrative recounts stands one level above the narrating act that produces that narrative. Take the "visiting reporter" version: the novel tells how, in 2025, a reporter visits Lin Lan, and Lin Lan tells the reporter how she found the old letter in 2020. There are three levels here. The **extradiegetic** level is the telling act that recounts "the reporter visits Lin Lan," the outermost layer of the whole novel. The **intradiegetic** (or diegetic) level is the first story world and its events: the reporter's visit and Lin Lan's beginning to speak both happen here. The **metadiegetic** level is the story told by a character within the story: Lin Lan's account of finding the letter in 2020. Compare another version in which the novel opens directly with "In 2025 I finally decided to tell you how I found the old letter," and Lin Lan carries the telling of the whole novel. **Now her telling stands at the extradiegetic level.** The two tellings could be word for word the same, yet their levels differ, because the structure surrounding them has changed.

> **故事里的人物也可以讲故事，讲出的故事里又可以有人讲故事。** *叙述层*描述的就是讲述行为在这种嵌套结构中的位置。热奈特的定义是：一个叙事所讲述的任何事件，都处在产生这一叙事的讲述行为的上一层。以“记者来访”版本为例：小说讲的是2025年一位记者拜访林岚，林岚向记者讲述2020年发现旧信的经过。这里有三个层次。**故事外层**：讲述“记者拜访林岚”这件事的那个讲述行为，位于整部小说的最外层。**故事内层**：第一层故事世界及其中的事件，记者来访、林岚开口讲述，都发生在这一层。**元故事层**：故事中的人物所讲出的故事，即林岚讲出的2020年发现旧信的经过。对照另一个版本：小说直接以“2025年，我终于决定把发现旧信的经过告诉你”开篇，由林岚承担整部小说的讲述。**此时她的讲述位于故事外层。** 两次讲述可以一字不差，层次却不同，因为包围它们的结构变了。

> - *narrative level*：叙述层。讲述行为在嵌套结构中的位置。
> - *extradiegetic* \[ˌekstrəˌdaɪəˈdʒetɪk\]：故事外层的。产生第一层叙事的讲述行为所在的位置。
> - *intradiegetic / diegetic*：故事内层的。第一层故事世界及其中的事件与讲述。
> - *metadiegetic*：元故事层的。故事中人物讲出的第二层故事。
> - *diegesis* \[ˌdaɪəˈdʒiːsɪs\]：n. 故事世界。热奈特用以指叙事所呈现的时空与事件整体。
> - 引文出处：Genette, *Narrative Discourse*, ch. 5；Pier, “Narrative Levels,” *LHN* (2016)。

Three common misunderstandings should be avoided. **First, "extradiegetic" does not mean the real world, nor does it mean the narrator took no part in the events.** Lin Lan can be both the extradiegetic teller and a participant in what she tells. The test is: **has this telling of hers already been presented as an event in the story by another telling?** That is far more precise than asking whether she is "inside or outside the story." **Second, the prefix "meta-" in "metadiegetic" indicates only the level of nesting, not reflexivity.** Genette himself admitted that the prefix runs in the opposite direction from its use in logic's "metalanguage" and that he chose it for convenience. **Third, quotation marks, memories, and dreams do not automatically add a level.** A character saying "Give me the letter" is simply a presented dialogue; only when a character begins to tell "how, that summer, I found my father locking the drawer" does she take on the function of an embedded telling. Whether a letter, a dream, or a memory forms an independent level depends on the actual structure, not on a change of layout, the use of the first person, or a reference to the past.

> 有三种常见误解需要避免。**第一，“故事外层”不等于现实世界，也不等于叙述者没有参与事件。** 林岚可以既是故事外层的讲述者，又是所讲事件的参与者。判断的标准是：**她的这次讲述，是否已经被另一个讲述当作故事中的事件来呈现？** 这比问“她在故事里面还是外面”精确得多。**第二，“元故事”的“元”只表示嵌套的层次，不表示“反思性”。** 热奈特自己也承认，这个前缀的方向与逻辑学中“元语言”的用法恰好相反，选用它只是图方便。**第三，引号、回忆、梦境不会自动增加叙述层。** 人物说“把信给我”，只是一次被呈现的对话；人物开始讲“那年夏天，我怎样发现父亲锁上抽屉”，才承担了嵌入讲述的功能。书信、梦境或回忆是否构成独立的层次，要看具体结构，而不是看排版变化、是否用了第一人称或是否涉及过去。

> - *reflexivity* \[ˌriːflekˈsɪvəti\]：n. 反思性；自我指涉性。
> - *metalanguage*：元语言。用来谈论另一种语言的语言，在逻辑学中“元”指高一层，而热奈特的“元故事”是低一层（被嵌入）的故事。
> - *embedded telling*：嵌入的讲述。

**What does an embedded story do for the outer level?** Genette distinguishes three relations. In an **explanatory** relation, the embedded story accounts for the origin of the outer situation, as when Lin Lan tells of her mother's past to explain why brother and sister quarrel today. In a **thematic** relation, the embedded story stands in similarity or contrast to the outer level without a direct causal link. In an **actional** relation, what matters is not what is told but **the act of telling itself**, which produces an effect at the outer level, as when Lin Lan's account of her mother's past persuades her brother not to burn the letter, so that storytelling becomes an act of persuasion. **The focus of analysis thus moves from "there is a story within a story" to "how telling a story becomes an action in the story."**

> **嵌入的故事对外层起什么作用？** 热奈特区分三种关系。**解释性**：嵌入的故事交代外层处境的来由，例如林岚讲母亲的过去，以解释今天姐弟为何争执。**主题性**：嵌入的故事与外层构成相似或对比，但没有直接的因果联系。**行动性**：要紧的不是讲了什么，而是**讲述这个行为本身**在外层产生了效果，例如林岚讲述母亲的往事，使弟弟放弃了烧掉那封信，讲故事由此成为一次劝说。**分析的重点由此从“这里有故事套故事”，推进到“讲故事如何成为故事中的一个行动”。**

> - *explanatory / thematic / actional*：解释性 / 主题性 / 行动性。嵌入叙事与外层叙事之间的三种功能关系。
> - *account for*：解释；说明……的原因。

**The traditional "first-person / third-person narration" mixes grammar with structure.** Genette asks a different question: **does the narrator appear as a character in the story she tells?** A **heterodiegetic** narrator is absent from the story told; a **homodiegetic** narrator is a character in it; and an **autodiegetic** narrator is a homodiegetic narrator who is also the protagonist (a witness or minor character telling the story is not autodiegetic). Crossing level (above) with participation gives four basic narrator situations:

- **Extradiegetic + heterodiegetic**: the novel's narrator has no connection with the Lin family.
- **Extradiegetic + homodiegetic**: the novel is told directly by Lin Lan looking back on her own discovery.
- **Intradiegetic + heterodiegetic**: within the story of the reporter's visit, Lin Lan tells of events before her birth in which she took no part.
- **Intradiegetic + homodiegetic**: within the story of the reporter's visit, Lin Lan tells of her own discovery years before.

> **传统的“第一人称／第三人称叙述”把语法和结构混在了一起。** 热奈特换了一个问法：**叙述者是否作为人物出现在她所讲的故事中？** **异故事叙述者**不在所讲的故事里；**同故事叙述者**是所讲故事中的一个人物；**自身故事叙述者**则是同时身为主人公的同故事叙述者（由见证者或配角来讲，不算自身故事叙述）。把上文的“层次”与这里的“参与关系”交叉，得到四种基本的叙述者情境：
>
> - **故事外层＋异故事**：小说的叙述者与林家毫无关系。
> - **故事外层＋同故事**：小说直接由林岚回顾自己发现旧信的经历。
> - **故事内层＋异故事**：在记者来访的故事中，林岚讲一段她出生之前、未曾参与的往事。
> - **故事内层＋同故事**：在记者来访的故事中，林岚讲自己当年的发现。

> - *heterodiegetic* \[ˌhetərəʊˌdaɪəˈdʒetɪk\]：异故事的。叙述者不是所讲故事中的人物。
> - *homodiegetic* \[ˌhɒməʊˌdaɪəˈdʒetɪk\]：同故事的。叙述者是所讲故事中的人物。
> - *autodiegetic* \[ˌɔːtəʊˌdaɪəˈdʒetɪk\]：自身故事的。叙述者即主人公。
> - *narrator situation*：叙述者情境，即层次与参与关系组合而成的类型。

When using this classification, first specify which stretch of narrative is being analyzed: a homodiegetic narrator who, in a long memoir, retells things that happened before her birth does not thereby turn the whole work into heterodiegetic narration. **Remember too that grammatical person cannot replace the judgment of participation.** A heterodiegetic narrator can interject "I must remind the reader" and still take no part in the events; a homodiegetic narrator may refer to her past self by her own name or as "she." Person has its stylistic and rhetorical value, but cannot by itself decide the narrator's type.

> 使用这一分类时，要先说明分析的是哪一段叙事：同故事叙述者在长篇回忆中转述自己出生前的事，并不会因此让整部作品变成异故事叙述。**还要记住，语法人称不能代替对参与关系的判断。** 异故事叙述者可以插一句“我必须提醒读者”，却依然不参与所讲的事件；同故事叙述者也可能用自己的名字或“她”来称呼过去的自己。人称有其文体和修辞上的价值，但不能单独决定叙述者的类型。

> - *grammatical person*：语法人称。
> - *interject* \[ˌɪntəˈdʒekt\]：v. 插话；插入（评论）。

**Where there is a teller, there is someone told.** The **narratee** (*narrataire*) is the recipient that the narration sets up or presupposes within the text, not the real reader holding the book. "Brother, I know you still believe our father" places the brother in an explicit narratee position, while the real reader overhears. Because the brother has his own knowledge, feelings, and stance, Lin Lan may skip what both know and dwell on something an ordinary reader would not find contentious. Even an unnamed narratee can be inferred from how fully things are explained, from forms of address, and from presupposed knowledge. Like narrators, narratees can be extradiegetic or intradiegetic. The narratee may also fail to coincide with whoever actually hears or reads the narration in the story: if an explanatory letter written to the brother is in the end read by the reporter, the intended and the actual recipient no longer match. Such mismatches help explain why information fails, how misunderstandings arise, and how private expression is taken as public testimony. **Do not identify every "you" with the real reader, and do not equate the narratee directly with the "implied reader" of other theories.**

> **有讲述者，就有被讲述的对象。** **受述者**（narrataire）是叙述在文本中设定或预设的接受者，不等于手里拿着书的现实读者。“弟弟，我知道你仍然相信父亲”把弟弟放在明确的受述位置，现实读者则在旁听。由于弟弟有自己已有的知识、情感和立场，林岚可能略过双方都熟悉的事，也可能反复解释一件普通读者并不觉得有争议的事。即使受述者没有被点名，也可以从解释的详略、称呼方式和预设的知识中推断出来。受述者与叙述者一样，也有故事外层与故事内层之分。受述者还可能与故事中实际听到或读到叙述的人不一致：一封写给弟弟的解释信，如果最终被记者读到，预定的接受者与实际的接受者就不再相同。这种错位可以用来分析信息为何失效、误解如何产生，以及私人表达怎样被当成公开证词。**不要把所有的“你”都认作现实读者，也不要把受述者与其他理论中的“隐含读者”直接等同。**

> - *narratee (narrataire)* \[ˌnærəˈtiː\]：受述者。叙述在文本内部所设定的接受者。
> - *overhear* \[ˌəʊvəˈhɪə(r)\]：v. 旁听；无意中听到。
> - *contentious* \[kənˈtenʃəs\]：adj. 有争议的。
> - *implied reader*：隐含读者。伊瑟尔等人的概念，指文本结构所预设的理想阅读者，与受述者不同。
> - 引文出处：Wolf Schmid, “Narratee,” *LHN* (2013)。

**The narrator does more than report events.** Genette distinguishes five functions, which often overlap within a single sentence. The **narrative function** tells what happened. The **directing function** organizes the text itself: "Let me first speak of my mother." The **communicative function** establishes or maintains contact with the narratee: "Brother, you must remember..." The **testimonial function** states the source of information, the accuracy of memory, or the narrator's emotional attitude: "I still remember those words clearly." The **ideological function** makes general judgments or comments: "Every family has things it cannot speak of." "You probably won't believe it, but I still remember those words clearly" has both a communicative and a testimonial function. **Functional analysis is especially suited to testing how narrative authority is built.** "I saw it with my own eyes" is only a claim to authority and does not prove the content true; "every family has things it cannot speak of" may dress up one family's problem as a universal law. In analysis, record **statements of fact, claims about evidence, and value judgments** separately, then see whether later passages support or dismantle them.

> **叙述者不只是交代事件。** 热奈特区分了五种功能，它们常常在一句话中重叠。**叙事功能**：讲述发生了什么。**管理功能**：组织文本本身，例如“下面我要先讲母亲。”**交流功能**：与受述者建立或维持联系，例如“弟弟，你一定记得……”**证明功能**：交代消息的来源、记忆的准确程度或自己的情感态度，例如“那句话我至今记得清清楚楚。”**意识形态功能**：作出一般性的判断或评论，例如“家家都有不能说的事。”“你大概不信，但那句话我至今记得清清楚楚”一句话就同时具有交流功能和证明功能。**功能分析特别适合用来检验叙述权威是怎样建立起来的。** “我亲眼看见”只是一种权威声明，并不能证明内容为真；“家家都有不能说的事”可能把一个家庭的问题包装成普遍规律。分析时应把**事实陈述、证据声明和价值判断**分开记录，再看后文是支持还是瓦解它们。

> - *narrative / directing / communicative / testimonial / ideological function*：叙事 / 管理 / 交流 / 证明 / 意识形态功能。
> - *narrative authority*：叙述权威。叙述者让读者相信其陈述与判断的那种地位。
> - *dress up as*：把……包装成。
> - *dismantle* \[dɪsˈmæntl\]：v. 拆解；瓦解。

## V. Combining the Dimensions; Metalepsis

Sections I–IV took narrative apart into time, mode, and voice. **The purpose of taking them apart is to recombine them: the dimensions acquire explanatory power only when they are put back together.** Compare two versions. In **Version A (memoir)**, Lin Lan narrates subsequently, at the extradiegetic level, as a homodiegetic and indeed autodiegetic narrator, with focalization confined mainly to her 2020 self who does not yet know the truth. In **Version B (present tense)**, a heterodiegetic narrator at the extradiegetic level tells in the present tense, with internal focalization on Lin Lan. Both versions keep the reader temporarily ignorant of the hiding of the letter, so their restriction of information is the same. But Version A raises a further question that Version B does not: **why is the present-day Lin Lan remembering in this way?** The motive of narrating becomes part of the work.

> 前四节把叙事拆成了时间、语式和语态等维度。**拆开是为了重新组合：各个维度只有重新放在一起，才具有解释力。** 比较两个版本。**版本甲（回忆体）**：林岚在故事外层，以同故事、并且是自身故事的身份进行事后叙述，聚焦主要限于2020年那个尚不知情的自己。**版本乙（现在时）**：一个异故事叙述者在故事外层用现在时讲述，内部聚焦于林岚。两个版本都让读者暂时不知道藏信的真相，信息限制是相同的。但版本甲多出了一个版本乙没有的问题：**如今的林岚为什么要这样回忆？** 叙述的动机本身成了作品的一部分。

> - *recombine* \[ˌriːkəmˈbaɪn\]：v. 重新组合。
> - *explanatory power*：解释力。
> - *motive of narrating*：叙述动机。叙述者为何讲、为谁讲、想达到什么目的。

This also shows that traditional labels such as "first-person / third-person" and "omniscient / limited" have not become useless. They are convenient shorthand; they simply bundle several dimensions together. **Genette's contribution is to let us unbundle and test them**: two third-person texts may impose entirely different restrictions on information, and of two first-person texts one may keep strictly to the experience of the time while the other keeps revealing outcomes in advance.

> 这也说明，“第一人称／第三人称”、“全知／限知”之类的传统说法并没有失效，它们是方便的简称，只是把好几个维度打包在了一起。**热奈特的贡献，在于让我们能把它们拆开来检验**：同样是第三人称，两段文本的信息限制可能截然不同；同样是第一人称，一段可能严守当时的经验，另一段却不断提前揭示结果。

> - *shorthand* \[ˈʃɔːthænd\]：n. 简称；速记。
> - *bundle / unbundle*：打包 / 拆开。

**Section IV established that the telling act and the told world lie on different levels and normally do not communicate**: characters cannot interfere with those who narrate them, and narrators cannot walk into the story they tell. ***Metalepsis*** names the breaking of this boundary, an intrusion between the world of telling and the world told, or between an outer story and an embedded one, that violates the normal hierarchy of levels. The term comes from classical rhetoric: rhetoricians called it a figure when one says "Virgil has Dido die," presenting the poet's describing an event as though the poet himself performed it. Genette borrowed it to describe a "short circuit" between narrative levels, and in his later monograph *Métalepse: From Figure to Fiction* (2004) extended it from a figure of speech into one of the ways fiction is constituted.

> **第四节已经说明，讲述行为与被讲述的世界分处不同层次，正常情况下互不相通**：故事中的人物不能干预讲述他们的人，讲述者也不能走进自己讲的故事。***叙事越界***（métalepse）指的就是这条边界被打破：讲述的世界与被讲述的世界之间、外层故事与嵌入故事之间，发生了违反正常层级关系的侵入。这个术语原本来自古典修辞学：修辞学家把“维吉尔让狄多死去”这种说法，即把诗人描写某事说成诗人亲手做了这件事，称为一种辞格。热奈特借用它来描述叙述层次之间的“短路”，并在后来的专著《转叙：从辞格到虚构》（2004）中，把它从一种修辞格扩展为虚构的一种构成方式。

> - *metalepsis (métalepse)* \[ˌmetəˈlepsɪs\]：叙事越界，也译“转叙”。叙述层次之间违反正常层级的侵入或跨越。
> - *intrusion* \[ɪnˈtruːʒn\]：n. 侵入；闯入。
> - *hierarchy of levels*：层级关系。
> - *figure of speech*：修辞格；辞格。
> - *short circuit*：短路。比喻不同层次被直接接通。
> - 引文出处：Pier, “Metalepsis,” *LHN* (2016)；Genette, *Métalepse: De la figure à la fiction* (Seuil, 2004)。

Suppose Lin Lan reads her mother's diary, which describes a rainstorm in 1998: that is a normal embedding. If the mother in the diary suddenly says, "Lin Lan, you who are reading this page, stop turning," and the text goes on to have the 1998 mother respond directly to the 2020 Lin Lan's movements, there is a metalepsis: **a character in the told world proves able to perceive and interfere with the reading going on at an outer level.** Three similar phenomena must be excluded when identifying metalepsis. **The author's act of creation is not itself metalepsis**: real authors decide their characters' fates as a matter of course; only when a work *writes into itself* such cross-level interference, creating a short circuit between levels, is there metalepsis. A character named "the Author" in a novel is not enough either, since he may be an ordinary character; one must show that he crosses the boundary between teller and told. **Metafiction is not metalepsis**: a novel that discusses its own writing is using a common device of metafiction but need not cross levels. **Mirror structures are not metalepsis**: an embedded story that mirrors the outer one, the so-called *mise en abyme*, need not cross levels either. Whether a character "looking into the camera" or addressing the reader directly counts as metalepsis depends on the medium and on the reception position the work establishes.

> 设想林岚读母亲的日记，日记写的是1998年的一场雨，这是正常的嵌入关系。如果日记中的母亲忽然说：“正在读这页纸的林岚，别再往下翻了”，而文本进一步让1998年的母亲直接回应2020年林岚的动作，就构成了越界：**被讲述世界中的人物，竟能察觉并干预外层的阅读活动。** 识别越界时，要排除三种相似的现象。**作者的创作行为本身不是越界**：现实作者本来就决定人物的命运；只有当作品把这种跨层的干预*写进作品本身*、造成层次之间的短路时，才是越界。小说中出现一个名叫“作者”的人物也不够，他可能只是一个普通角色；必须证明他跨越了讲述者与被讲述者之间的边界。**元小说不等于越界**：一部小说讨论自己的写作过程，是元小说的常见手法，但未必跨越层次。**镜像结构不等于越界**：嵌入的故事与外层故事相互映照，即所谓“*纹心结构*”，也未必跨层。角色“看向镜头”或直接对读者说话是否算越界，要结合媒介和作品所设定的接受位置来判断。

> - *write into itself*：写进作品本身。指越界必须是作品内部呈现出来的结构，而非创作的一般事实。
> - *metafiction* \[ˌmetəˈfɪkʃn\]：元小说。在作品中暴露、讨论自身虚构性与写作过程的小说。
> - *mise en abyme* \[ˌmiːz ɒn əˈbiːm\]：（法语）纹心结构；镜渊结构。作品内部嵌入一个缩影般映照整体的局部。
> - *reception position*：接受位置。作品为观众或读者设定的观看、阅读的位置。

**Metalepsis can produce comedy, and it can produce unease.** That the mother of long ago could read her daughter's reading in return unsettles the comfortable belief that "the past is over and a record is only a record." Once a metalepsis has been identified, go on to ask: **Which boundary has been broken? Who has thereby gained a power to act they did not have before? Does the work sustain the impossibility, or does it finally offer an ordinary explanation?**

> **越界可以制造喜剧，也可以制造不安。** 旧日的母亲竟能反过来读懂女儿的阅读，这会动摇“过去已经结束、记录只是记录”的安全感。识别出越界之后，还应继续追问：**打破的是哪条边界？谁因此获得了原本没有的行动能力？作品是维持这种不可能，还是最终给出一个寻常的解释？**

> - *unease* \[ʌnˈiːz\]：n. 不安；忧虑。
> - *unsettle* \[ʌnˈsetl\]：v. 动摇；使不安。
> - *sustain* \[səˈsteɪn\]：v. 维持；支撑。

## VI. Transtextuality: How a Work Depends on Other Texts

Sections I–V concerned how a work tells from the inside. From the late 1970s Genette turned to another question: **how does a work come into being with the help of other texts?** He used ***transtextuality*** for every relation between one text and others, distinguished five types at the opening of *Palimpsests* (1982), and devoted the rest of that book to one of them, hypertextuality. The title refers to medieval parchment scraped clean and written over, on which traces of the older writing remain faintly visible, an apt image for a work beneath which other works lie layered. The five relations can coexist in one work; they are not a classification from which only one may be chosen.

- **Intertextuality**: another text is **actually present** in this one, through quotation, plagiarism, or allusion. The mother's letter quotes an existing poem.
- **Paratextuality**: the relation between the text and the materials that **surround and present** it, such as title, signature, and preface. The cover reads *A Mother's True Departure*.
- **Metatextuality**: the relation of **commentary** of one text upon another. A review analyzes the self-justification in the mother's letters.
- **Hypertextuality**: a later text **derives** from an earlier one by transformation or imitation. A new novel systematically rewrites an existing story of a departure.
- **Architextuality**: the relation of a text to **general categories** such as genre, type of discourse, and mode of enunciation. The novel borrows the conventions of the epistolary novel, the family saga, or the detective story.

> 前五节讨论的是一部作品如何从内部进行讲述。从1970年代末起，热奈特转向另一个问题：**一部作品怎样借助其他文本而得以成立？** 他用***跨文本性***统称一个文本与其他文本之间的一切关系，在《隐迹稿本》（1982）开篇区分出五种类型，随后用全书的篇幅集中研究其中的“超文本性”。书名指中世纪刮去旧字、重新书写的羊皮纸，旧字的痕迹仍隐约可见，恰好比喻一部作品下面叠着其他作品。这五种关系可以同时出现在一部作品中，并不是只能择一的分类。
>
> - **互文性**：另一文本**实际出现**在这个文本中，如引用、抄袭、典故。例：母亲的信中引用了一首现成的诗。
> - **副文本性**：标题、署名、序言等**包裹并呈现**正文的材料与正文之间的关系。例：封面题为《一个母亲的真实出走》。
> - **元文本性**：一个文本对另一文本的**评论**关系。例：一篇书评分析母亲书信中的自我辩护。
> - **超文本性**：后来的文本通过改写或模仿从先前的文本**派生**而来。例：一部新小说系统地重写一个现成的出走故事。
> - **类属文本性**：文本与体裁、话语类型、陈述模式等**一般类别**之间的关系。例：小说借用了书信体、家族小说或侦探小说的惯例。

> - *transtextuality* \[ˌtrænzˌtekstʃuˈæləti\]：跨文本性。一个文本与其他文本之间一切关系的总称。
> - *palimpsest* \[ˈpælɪmpsest\]：n. 隐迹稿本；重写本。刮去旧字后重新书写、旧迹仍可辨的羊皮纸。
> - *intertextuality / paratextuality / metatextuality / hypertextuality / architextuality*：互文性 / 副文本性 / 元文本性 / 超文本性 / 类属文本性。
> - *allusion* \[əˈluːʒn\]：n. 典故；暗指。
> - *epistolary novel* \[ɪˈpɪstələri\]：书信体小说。
> - 引文出处：Genette, *Palimpsests: Literature in the Second Degree*, trans. Channa Newman & Claude Doubinsky (U of Nebraska P, 1997)。

**Three terms need special comment.** "Architextuality" is sometimes rendered as "archetypal textuality," but the *architexte* is neither an older work serving as a prototype nor an archetype in Jungian psychology; it is the general category (genre, kind) to which a work belongs, which is why this guide uses "类属." "Hypertextuality" has nothing to do with web hyperlinks, and "metatextuality" does not mean the metafiction of a novel that reflects on its own writing. **Genette's "intertextuality" is much narrower than Julia Kristeva's.** Kristeva held that every text is a mosaic of quotations from other texts, so that intertextuality is a universal property of texts; Genette uses the word only for identifiable, actual co-presence. Whenever you use the term, say which sense you mean.

> **有三个术语需要特别说明。** “类属文本性”有时译作“原型文本性”，但 architexte 既不是某一部充当原型的旧作品，也不是荣格心理学中的“原型”，而是作品所属的一般类别（体裁、文类等），所以本文用“类属”二字以免误解。“超文本性”与网页超链接无关；“元文本性”也不是指小说反思自身写作的“元小说”。**热奈特的“互文性”比克里斯蒂娃的用法窄得多。** 克里斯蒂娃认为一切文本都是由其他文本的引文编织而成，互文性是文本的普遍属性；热奈特只用它指可以识别的、实际的共现关系。使用这个词时，应说明采用的是哪一种含义。

> - *architexte*：（法语）类属文本。作品所属的体裁、话语类型等一般类别，而非具体的旧作。
> - *archetype* \[ˈɑːkitaɪp\]：n. 原型。荣格心理学中指集体无意识中的普遍意象。
> - *mosaic of quotations*：引文的马赛克。克里斯蒂娃对文本的描述，强调一切文本都是对其他文本的吸收与转化。
> - *co-presence*：共现。一个文本中实际出现另一文本的片段。

**Intertextuality requires a connection that can be checked; it cannot rest on "this reminds me of another work."** Two novels that both feature a letter may simply share a common motif; only distinctive wording, a similar configuration of plot, or a culturally unmistakable allusion counts as solid evidence. An allusion need not quote the original sentence in full, but one must say how it is recognized. Metatextuality concerns only the relation of commentary; it does not require the commented text to be quoted in full or even named. A review that both quotes the novel and analyzes its prejudices has an intertextual and a metatextual relation at once: these are two aspects of the same connection, not two mutually exclusive identities.

> **互文性的判定需要可以核查的关联，不能止于“这让我想起另一部作品”。** 两部小说都写了一封信，可能只是共用一个常见母题；只有出现独特的措辞、相似的情节配置，或文化上足够明确的暗示，才算是较扎实的证据。暗示不必完整引用原句，但需要说明凭什么认出它。元文本性只关注评论关系，不要求被评论的文本被完整引用，甚至不要求点名。一篇书评如果既引用了小说原句、又分析其中的偏见，就同时具有互文关系与元文本关系，这是同一种联系的两个方面，而不是两个互相排斥的身份。

> - *motif* \[məʊˈtiːf\]：n. 母题；主题；图案。
> - *configuration* \[kənˌfɪɡəˈreɪʃn\]：n. 配置；构造；布局。
> - *mutually exclusive*：互相排斥的。

**The earlier text that is rewritten or imitated is the *hypotext*; the later work derived from it is the *hypertext*.** Derivation has two basic operations. **Transformation** alters the material or structure of the earlier work directly, as when a story of "the daughter's departure" becomes "the mother's departure," keeping the main relations among characters while changing identities and ending; the question here is **how has this story been reworked?** **Imitation** extracts a model of style or manner from the earlier work and uses it to write a new text, as when an unrelated story is written throughout in the old work's sentence patterns, tone, and rhetorical habits; the question here is **how has this manner of writing been reproduced?** A work can use both. Genette crosses operation with three *functional tendencies* (playful, satirical, serious) to obtain six basic positions:

- **Transformation + playful**: parody (*parodie*).
- **Transformation + satirical**: travesty (*travestissement*).
- **Transformation + serious**: transposition (*transposition*).
- **Imitation + playful**: pastiche (*pastiche*).
- **Imitation + satirical**: caricature (*charge*).
- **Imitation + serious**: forgery (*forgerie*).

> **被改写或模仿的先前文本称为*底本*，由它派生出的后作称为*超文本*。** 派生有两种基本操作。**转换**直接改动前作的材料或结构，例如把“女儿出走”的故事改成“母亲出走”，保留主要的人物关系，调整人物身份和结局；这里要问的是**这个故事被怎样改造了？** **模仿**从前作中提炼出风格或写法的模式，再用它写出一个新文本，例如另写一个与旧作情节无关的故事，却通篇模仿旧作的句式、口吻和修辞习惯；这里要问的是**这套写法被怎样再生产了？** 一部作品可以兼用两种操作。热奈特把“操作”与三种*功能倾向*（游戏性、讽刺性、严肃性）交叉，得到六个基本位置：
>
> - **转换＋游戏性**：戏仿（parodie）。
> - **转换＋讽刺性**：滑稽改写（travestissement）。
> - **转换＋严肃性**：严肃转换（transposition）。
> - **模仿＋游戏性**：风格仿作（pastiche）。
> - **模仿＋讽刺性**：讽刺性仿作（charge）。
> - **模仿＋严肃性**：严肃仿作（forgerie）。

> - *hypotext / hypertext*：底本 / 超文本。前者是被派生的先前文本，后者是派生出来的后作。
> - *transformation / imitation*：转换 / 模仿。前者改动前作的故事或结构，后者再生产前作的风格模式。
> - *functional tendency*：功能倾向。游戏、讽刺、严肃三种态度。
> - *parody / travesty / transposition / pastiche / caricature / forgery*：戏仿 / 滑稽改写 / 严肃转换 / 风格仿作 / 讽刺性仿作 / 严肃仿作。

These six are **the intersection of two axes of judgment, not six sharply bounded types of work**; Genette himself admits that the three functions form a continuous spectrum with mixed forms. Two further points: *parodie* here means specifically a playful rewriting of an earlier work, not the everyday sense of mockery or spoof; and *pastiche* means stylistic imitation, not collage. *Forgerie* does not mean fraudulent forgery in real life but a continuation of an established manner in a serious spirit, such as composing new books for a classical epic.

> 这六个位置是**两条判断轴的交叉，而不是六种界限分明的作品类型**；热奈特本人也承认三种功能之间是一个连续的光谱，可以有混合形态。还需注意两点：这里的 parodie 特指对前作的游戏性改写，不等于日常所说的“恶搞、嘲弄”；pastiche 是风格模仿，也不等于“拼贴”。forgerie 并非现实中的伪造欺诈，而是以严肃态度延续某种既有写法的拟作，例如为古典史诗续写新的篇章。

> - *two axes of judgment*：两条判断轴。
> - *spectrum* \[ˈspektrəm\]：n. 光谱；范围；连续体。
> - *spoof* \[spuːf\]：n. 恶搞；滑稽模仿。
> - *collage* \[ˈkɒlɑːʒ\]：n. 拼贴。

**Serious transposition is the largest and most varied of the six.** Genette subdivides it into many formal and thematic operations, of which the most commonly useful are these. On the formal side: translation; versification and prosification; reduction (cutting, condensing) and augmentation (expansion, addition); and **transmodalization**, a shift between narrative and dramatic presentation, as when a novel is adapted for the stage. On the thematic side: **diegetic transposition**, which changes the time, place, or social identity of the story; **transmotivation**, which keeps a character's action but replaces its motive; and **transvaluation**, which changes how a character or action is valued. The last two are especially important. If the original attributes the mother's leaving to selfishness, a rewriting can keep the act of "leaving" while adding the long oppression she endured, so that the same act receives an entirely different explanation. What changes then is not only the ending but **why the action makes sense and who has the right to judge it**.

> **严肃转换是六类中篇幅最大、类型最多的一类。** 热奈特把它细分为形式层面与主题层面的多种操作，其中分析时最常用的如下。形式层面：翻译；韵文改写为散文或散文改写为韵文；缩减（删节、浓缩）与扩充（扩写、增补）；以及**转换模式**，即在“叙述”与“戏剧呈现”之间转换，例如把小说改编为剧本。主题层面：**故事世界移置**，改变故事发生的时代、地点或人物的社会身份；**动机转换**，保留人物的行动而替换其动机；**价值转换**，改变对人物或行动的价值评价。后两种尤其关键。原作把母亲离家归因于自私，改写可以保留“离开”这一行动，却补写她长期承受的压迫，使同一行为得到完全不同的解释。此时改变的不只是结局，而是**这个行动为何成立、谁有资格评价它**。

> - *versification / prosification*：韵文化 / 散文化。
> - *reduction / augmentation*：缩减 / 扩充。
> - *transmodalization*：转换模式。在叙述模式与戏剧模式之间转换。
> - *diegetic transposition*：故事世界移置。改变故事的时空背景或人物身份。
> - *transmotivation*：动机转换。保留行动、替换动机。
> - *transvaluation*：价值转换。改变对人物或行动的评价。

When comparing an earlier and a later work, a useful sentence pattern is: "**The later work keeps X from the earlier one and changes Y, so that judgments formerly resting on Z are unsettled.**" For example: it keeps the mother's departure but moves the narrative center from the husband to the daughter and gradually presents the mother's own letters, so that the evidence and voices available to the reader change. This inference still has to be verified against the two works in detail; it cannot be completed with the two words "female perspective."

> 比较前作与后作时，可以使用这样一个句式：“**后作保留了前作的X，改变了Y，因此原先建立在Z之上的判断受到了动摇。**”例如：保留母亲出走，却把叙述中心从丈夫移到女儿，并逐步呈现母亲自己的书信，读者能接触到的证据和声音因此改变。这一推论仍需在两部作品的具体对照中验证，不能只凭“女性视角”四个字完成。

> - *sentence pattern*：句式。
> - *inference* \[ˈɪnfərəns\]：n. 推论；推断。

***Introduction to the Architext*** (1979) asks where literary classification itself comes from. Genette distinguishes **modes of enunciation**, basic ways of presenting such as narration (told by a teller) and dramatic presentation (acted out by characters), from **historically formed genres** such as tragedy, the novel, or the sonnet. One of his central claims is that the triad "lyric, epic, dramatic," often credited to Aristotle or even Plato, actually took shape only gradually from the late eighteenth century into the Romantic period; Plato and Aristotle were mainly discussing modes of enunciation, not the three great genres in the later sense. **Many classifications that seem self-evident have a history of their own.**

> ***《类属文本导论》***（1979）追问文学分类本身的来历。热奈特区分两种东西：一是**陈述模式**，即叙述（由一个讲述者讲出来）与戏剧呈现（由人物直接演出来）等基本的表达方式；二是**历史形成的体裁**，如悲剧、小说、十四行诗等在特定历史中形成的类别。他的核心论点之一是：常被归功于亚里士多德甚至柏拉图的“抒情、史诗、戏剧”三分法，其实是从18世纪末到浪漫主义时期才逐步定型的；柏拉图和亚里士多德讨论的主要是陈述模式，而不是后来意义上的三大体裁。**许多看似天经地义的分类，其实都有自己的历史。**

> - *mode of enunciation*：陈述模式。叙述、戏剧呈现等基本的表达方式。
> - *historically formed genre*：历史形成的体裁。
> - *triad* \[ˈtraɪæd\]：n. 三分法；三元组。
> - *self-evident*：不言自明的；天经地义的。
> - 引文出处：Genette, *The Architext: An Introduction*, trans. Jane E. Lewin (U of California P, 1992)。

This affects actual reading. Read *The Unsent Letter* as a detective story, and the reader asks who hid the letter, whether the clues are fair, and whether the ending solves the puzzle; read it as a family novel, and the reader cares more about generational memory and how kinship persists. A work can call up both sets of expectations and set them in conflict, for instance borrowing the structure of an investigation while leaving the crucial truth forever unconfirmable, so that "investigating" itself becomes the theme. **Genre analysis should therefore not stop at a label** ("this is an epistolary psychological novel") but explain which conventions the work invokes, what those conventions lead readers to expect, and where the work satisfies, mixes, or changes those expectations. Note the distinction: "A Novel" printed on the cover is a paratextual marker, while the work's actual relation to the category "novel" is the architextual relation; the two may agree or be in tension.

> 这会影响实际的阅读。把《未寄出的信》当作侦探小说来读，读者会追问谁藏了信、线索是否公平、结尾有没有解开谜团；当作家族小说来读，读者更关心代际记忆和亲情如何延续。一部作品可以同时调动这两组期待，并让它们彼此冲突，例如借用调查的结构，却让关键真相永远无法确认，使“追查”本身成为主题。**因此，体裁分析不应止于贴标签**（“这是一部书信体心理小说”），而要说明作品调用了哪些惯例、这些惯例让读者期待什么，以及作品在哪里满足、混合或改变了这些期待。还须区分：封面上印着“长篇小说”是一个副文本标记；作品与“小说”这一类别的实际关系，才是类属文本关系。两者可以吻合，也可以有张力。

> - *call up*：唤起；调动。
> - *invoke* \[ɪnˈvəʊk\]：v. 调用；援引；诉诸。
> - *paratextual marker*：副文本标记，如封面上的体裁标注。

**The *paratext* is the set of materials surrounding the text that let it be presented and received as "a book."** In *Seuils* (*Thresholds*, 1987) Genette compares it to a *threshold*, neither wholly inside nor wholly outside. By position it divides into the **peritext**, printed in the same volume as the text (title, author's name, cover, epigraph, preface, notes), and the **epitext**, circulating outside the book (interviews, letters or diaries that discuss the work, publicity). If an interview is later included as an appendix in a new edition, its position and its role both change. The paratext is not decoration, but neither does it necessarily give the final interpretation of the text. Titled *The Unsent Letter*, the same novel directs attention to "a message that never arrived"; titled *Mother's Secret*, it stresses concealment; titled *Why She Stayed*, it turns "staying" in advance into something to be explained. **Without a word of the text changed, three titles lead readers to ask different questions.** This yields a practical method, the **substitution experiment**: imagine a different title, see which expectations change with it, and then return to the actual edition to look for evidence.

> ***副文本***是围绕正文、使它能以“一本书”的形式被呈现和接受的那些材料。热奈特在《门槛》（1987）中把它比作文本的*门槛*，既不完全在里面，也不完全在外面。按位置可以分为两类：**内副文本**，与正文印在同一本书里，如标题、署名、封面、题词、序言、注释；**外副文本**，在书外流通，如作者访谈、谈论作品的书信或日记、出版宣传等。同一篇访谈后来被收入新版附录，它的位置和作用都会随之改变。副文本不是装饰，但也不一定提供正文的最终解释。同一部小说，题为《未寄出的信》，会把注意力引向“消息没有送达”；题为《母亲的秘密》，会强化“隐瞒”；题为《她为什么留下》，则预先把“留下”变成一个需要解释的问题。**正文一字不改，三个标题就会引导读者提出不同的问题。** 由此得到一种实用的方法，即**替换实验**：设想换一个标题，看哪些期待随之改变，再回到实际版本中寻找证据。

> - *paratext* \[ˈpærətekst\]：副文本。
> - *threshold* \[ˈθreʃhəʊld\]：n. 门槛。热奈特以此说明副文本处于文本内外之间的过渡地带。
> - *peritext / epitext* \[ˈperɪtekst\] / \[ˈepɪtekst\]：内副文本 / 外副文本。
> - *epigraph* \[ˈepɪɡrɑːf\]：n. 题词；卷首引语。
> - *substitution experiment*：替换实验。替换某一副文本要素，观察读者期待随之发生的变化。
> - 引文出处：Genette, *Paratexts: Thresholds of Interpretation*, trans. Jane E. Lewin (Cambridge UP, 1997)；法文原版 *Seuils* (1987)。

Genette designed five questions for the study of paratexts, which can serve directly as a checklist. **Where?** Its spatial position, inside the book (a preface) or outside it (an interview). **When?** Its temporal relation to the first edition: before, simultaneous with, later than, or posthumous; a first-edition preface and a new afterword twenty years on work very differently. **In what form?** Verbal, iconic, material, or even "factual": an author's gender or prizes can affect reading as a paratext does. **From whom, to whom?** An author's preface, a publisher's band, and a translator's note are not the same voice and do not address the same readers. **To what end?** For example, indicating genre, guiding interpretation, or establishing authority. Genette stresses that the core of the paratext consists of materials **for which the author, or someone authorized by the author, is responsible**. An independent review thus does not become an epitext merely because it discusses the work outside the book; but if the publisher prints a line of its praise on the back cover, that line acquires a paratextual function.

> 热奈特为副文本研究设计了五个问题，可以直接用作分析清单。**在哪里？** 空间位置：在书内（如序言）还是书外（如访谈）。**何时出现？** 与初版的时间关系：先于初版、与初版同时、晚于初版，还是在作者身后；初版序言与二十年后新版后记的作用很不一样。**以什么形态？** 文字、图像、物质形态，乃至“事实”本身，作者的性别或获奖经历，也可能像副文本一样影响阅读。**由谁发出，给谁看？** 作者的序言、出版社的腰封、译者的说明，不是同一个声音，面向的读者也不同。**起什么作用？** 例如指示体裁、引导解释、建立权威。热奈特强调，副文本的核心是**由作者本人或经其认可者负责**的材料。因此，一篇独立的书评并不会仅仅因为在书外谈论作品就成为外副文本；但如果出版社把其中一句赞语印到封底，那句话就获得了副文本的功能。

> - *checklist*：分析清单；检查表。
> - *posthumous* \[ˈpɒstjʊməs\]：adj. 身后的；死后出版的。
> - *iconic* \[aɪˈkɒnɪk\]：adj. 图像的；形象的。
> - *publisher's band*：腰封。
> - *authorized* \[ˈɔːθəraɪzd\]：adj. 经授权的；经认可的。

The paratext also affects how we understand "fact." Suppose the cover says "a novel" while the preface claims the material comes from a trunk of real family letters: the work then wavers between fiction and testimony, and the analyst should record the wavering faithfully rather than resolve it on the work's behalf. **Editions are evidence too**: if two editions carry different prefaces, the expectations produced by one edition cannot be described as an unchanging property of the work.

> 副文本还会影响我们怎样理解“事实”。假设封面标注“小说”，前言却声称材料来自一箱真实的家书，作品就会在“虚构”与“见证”之间摇摆；分析者应如实记录这种摇摆，而不是替作品把它消除。**版本本身也是证据**：如果两个版本的序言不同，就不能把某一版造成的阅读预期，说成作品一成不变的性质。

> - *waver* \[ˈweɪvə(r)\]：v. 摇摆；犹豫。
> - *on someone's behalf*：代表某人；替某人。
> - *edition* \[ɪˈdɪʃn\]：n. 版本；版次。

## VII. Rhetoric, Verisimilitude, Mimology, and Style

**Beyond narrative structure, Genette kept asking another question: how does linguistic form itself enter literary experience?** This line of inquiry runs from his rhetorical studies of the 1960s to his theory of style in the 1990s, yet surveys that cover only narratology often leave it out.

> **在叙事结构之外，热奈特一直在追问另一个问题：语言形式本身怎样进入文学经验？** 这条线索从他1960年代的修辞学研究，一直延续到1990年代的文体理论，却常常被只讲叙事学的概述遗漏。

> - *line of inquiry*：探究的线索；研究方向。
> - *narratology* \[ˌnærəˈtɒlədʒi\]：叙事学。

In "Restricted Rhetoric" (1970), Genette traced the *shrinking* of rhetoric. Classical rhetoric was a complete art of persuasion with three main parts: **invention** (*inventio*), finding arguments; **arrangement** (*dispositio*), ordering the argument; and **style** (*elocutio*), choosing apt wording. Over time rhetoric shrank to the study of style alone, then to the study of *figures* (metaphor, repetition, antithesis, and the like), and finally to little more than metaphor and its paired counterpart metonymy. Genette criticized this *metaphor-centrism* for stripping rhetoric of its original breadth. **The criticism is a useful reminder in everyday reading.** Recognizing "the drawer was like a tightly shut mouth" as a simile does not complete a linguistic analysis. Word order, sentence length, pauses, repetition, register, mode of argument, and the readers addressed may all determine what effect a passage has. Figures are one analyzable aspect, not the whole of linguistic form.

> 在《受限的修辞学》（1970）一文中，热奈特梳理了修辞学的*萎缩*史。古典修辞学原本是一门完整的说服技艺，包括三大部分：**觅材**（inventio），找到论据；**布局**（dispositio），安排论证的结构；**表达**（elocutio），选择恰当的措辞。后来，修辞学逐渐收缩为只研究“表达”，再收缩为只研究*辞格*（比喻、反复、对偶等），最后几乎只剩下对隐喻以及与之对举的换喻的研究。热奈特批评这种*隐喻中心主义*使修辞学丧失了原有的广度。**这一批评对日常阅读也是有益的提醒。** 认出“抽屉像一张闭紧的嘴”是一个比喻，并不等于完成了语言分析。同一段文字的词序、句子长短、停顿、重复、语体、论证方式以及所面向的读者，都可能决定它产生什么效果。辞格只是可分析的一个方面，而不是语言形式的全部。

> - *shrinking*：n. 萎缩；收缩。
> - *invention / arrangement / style (inventio / dispositio / elocutio)*：觅材 / 布局 / 表达。古典修辞学的三大部分（完整体系另有记忆与发表两部分）。
> - *figure*：辞格。
> - *metonymy* \[məˈtɒnəmi\]：n. 换喻；转喻。以相关事物代替本体，如以“白宫”指美国政府。
> - *metaphor-centrism*：隐喻中心主义。
> - *register* \[ˈredʒɪstə(r)\]：n. 语体；语域。
> - 引文出处：Genette, “La rhétorique restreinte,” *Communications* 16 (1970): 158–71。

"Verisimilitude and Motivation" (1968) begins from a seventeenth-century French literary quarrel. In the novel *The Princess of Clèves*, the princess confesses to her husband that she loves another man, and many readers of the time judged this "not verisimilar." Genette shows that ***verisimilitude*** (*vraisemblance*) here measured not conformity to fact but conformity to a period's **public opinion** and **codes of propriety**: "a respectable woman does not do such a thing." **"She could not possibly do that" is thus sometimes an empirical judgment, and sometimes "she *ought not* to do that" disguised as one.** On this basis Genette divides narratives into three kinds. A **verisimilar narrative** has characters behave in accordance with public opinion, needs no further explanation, and is accepted naturally. A **motivated narrative** has behavior depart from the norm, so the narrative supplies explanations, psychological analysis or maxim-like commentary, to make it seem reasonable. An **arbitrary narrative** has behavior depart from the norm without any explanation at all.

> 《逼真性与动机》（1968）从17世纪法国的一场文学争论谈起：小说《克莱芙王妃》中，王妃向丈夫坦白自己爱上了别人，当时许多读者认为这“不逼真”。热奈特指出，这里所说的***逼真性***（vraisemblance）衡量的并不是是否符合事实，而是是否符合一个时代的**公共意见**与**礼仪规范**：“正经女人不会这样做。”**所以，“她不可能这样做”有时是一个经验判断，有时却是把“她*不应该*这样做”伪装成了经验判断。** 据此，热奈特把叙事分为三类。**逼真的叙事**：人物的行为符合公共意见，不需要额外解释，读者自然接受。**有动机的叙事**：行为偏离常规，于是叙事补上解释，如心理分析或格言式的评论，让它显得合理。**任意的叙事**：行为偏离常规，叙事却不作任何解释。

> - *verisimilitude (vraisemblance)* \[ˌverɪsɪˈmɪlɪtjuːd\]：逼真性。符合一个时代公共意见与礼仪规范的“可信”，而非符合事实的“真实”。
> - *public opinion*：公共意见。
> - *codes of propriety*：礼仪规范；得体的准则。
> - *empirical* \[ɪmˈpɪrɪkl\]：adj. 经验的；基于观察的。
> - *maxim* \[ˈmæksɪm\]：n. 格言；准则。
> - *verisimilar / motivated / arbitrary narrative*：逼真的 / 有动机的 / 任意的叙事。
> - 引文出处：Genette, “Vraisemblance et motivation,” *Communications* 11 (1968): 5–21。

His deeper argument is that **motivation often masks function.** A plot element frequently appears because the structure of the story needs it (its "what for"), while the work supplies a seemingly natural cause (its "why") that keeps readers from noticing the arbitrariness of the arrangement. Applied to the case: the father's hiding of the letter can be explained by jealousy, fear of losing his family, or concern for his reputation. Analysis should separate three things: **the reasons the character gives, the reasons the narrator supplies, and the conditions set up so that the plot will work**. The three may coincide or expose one another. If the narration keeps insisting "how could a mother be willing to leave her children," it is worth asking whether that sentence passes off a social norm as a psychological fact; if the mother's letter offers a different experience, the work may make an action once deemed "impossible" understandable again. **This is one entrance from formal analysis into gender, ethical, and social criticism**, though the specific historical norms still have to be researched separately.

> 他更深一层的论点是：**动机往往在掩饰功能。** 一个情节元素之所以出现，常常是因为故事结构需要它（它的“为了什么”）；作品却给它配上一个看似自然的原因（它的“为什么”），使读者察觉不到这种安排的任意性。用到案例上：父亲藏信，可以解释为嫉妒、害怕失去家人或维护名誉。分析时应区分三种东西：**人物自己给出的理由、叙述者提供的理由，以及为了让情节成立而设置的条件**。三者可能一致，也可能彼此拆穿。如果叙述反复强调“一个母亲怎么会愿意离开孩子”，就值得追问：这句话是否把一条社会规范当成了心理事实？如果母亲的信提供了不同的经验，作品就可能让原本被视为“不可能”的行动重新变得可以理解。**这是形式分析通向性别、伦理与社会批评的一个入口**，不过具体的历史规范仍需另行考证。

> - *motivation masks function*：动机掩饰功能。情节元素实际服务于结构需要，作品却为它提供看似自然的原因。
> - *arbitrariness* \[ˈɑːbɪtrərɪnəs\]：n. 任意性。
> - *pass off ... as ...*：把……冒充为……

***Mimologics*** (1976) traces an ancient desire: that the sounds, letter shapes, or structures of language should **resemble** what they refer to, as "cuckoo" imitates the bird's call. The question goes back to Plato's dialogue *Cratylus*: is the bond between name and thing natural or conventional? Cratylus in that dialogue holds the former, so Genette calls the position that names should naturally resemble things ***Cratylism***, and follows its history from antiquity through Enlightenment theories of the origin of language to poets such as Mallarmé and Claudel. A key distinction is between **primary Cratylism**, the belief that language already resembles things, and **secondary Cratylism**, exemplified by Mallarmé, which admits that ordinary language is arbitrary and unlike things, yet hopes that poetry will **make up for** this defect and let language become "like" things again in the poem.

> ***《拟仿论》***（1976）追踪一个古老的愿望：希望语言的声音、字形或结构**相似于**它所指的事物，就像“布谷”模仿布谷鸟的叫声那样。这个问题可以追溯到柏拉图的对话《克拉底鲁篇》：名称与事物之间，是自然的联系，还是人为的约定？对话中的克拉底鲁主张前者，热奈特因此把“名称应当自然地相似于事物”的立场称为***克拉底鲁主义***，并追溯它从古代、经启蒙时代的语言起源论，一直到马拉美、克洛岱尔等诗人的历史。其中一个关键区分是：**初级克拉底鲁主义**相信语言本来就与事物相似；**次级克拉底鲁主义**以马拉美为代表，承认日常语言是任意的、并不像事物，却寄望诗歌来**弥补**这一缺陷，让语言在诗中重新变得“像”事物。

> - *mimologics (mimologiques)* \[ˌmɪməˈlɒdʒɪks\]：拟仿论。研究“语言模仿事物”这一观念的历史。
> - *Cratylism* \[ˈkrætɪlɪzəm\]：克拉底鲁主义。认为名称与事物之间存在自然相似关系的立场。
> - *conventional* \[kənˈvenʃənl\]：adj. 约定俗成的；惯例的。
> - *primary / secondary Cratylism*：初级 / 次级克拉底鲁主义。
> - *make up for*：弥补。
> - 引文出处：Genette, *Mimologics*, trans. Thaïs E. Morgan (U of Nebraska P, 1995)；法文原版 *Mimologiques: Voyage en Cratylie* (1976)。

Genette does not claim that all words naturally imitate their objects; he studies the history and logic of the desire itself. For textual analysis this calls for distinguishing three strengths of claim. Take a passage describing the opening of the letter in short sentences: "She stopped. Reached out. Drew back." **It is fair to say** that the broken sentences and the hesitant movement form a perceptible correspondence in this passage. **It needs argument** on which cultural and stylistic conventions readers depend in order to feel that correspondence. **It is not acceptable to say** that short sentences naturally stand for fear in every language. In analyzing effects of resemblance in onomatopoeia, sound patterning, letter shapes, and syntax, always distinguish **the effect the work produces, the cultural convention behind it, and strong claims about the nature of language**.

> 热奈特并不主张所有词语都天然模仿其对象；他研究的是这种愿望本身的历史与逻辑。对文本分析而言，这提醒我们区分三种强度不同的说法。以一段用短句描写拆信的文字为例：“她停住。伸手。又缩回去。”**可以说**：断裂的句子与犹豫的动作，在这段文字中形成了可以感受到的对应。**需要论证**：读者要依赖哪些文化和文体惯例，才能感受到这种对应。**不能说**：短句在任何语言中都天然代表恐惧。分析拟声、音韵、字形和句法上的相似效果时，都应区分**作品造成的效果、背后的文化惯例，以及关于语言本质的强断言**。

> - *perceptible* \[pəˈseptəbl\]：adj. 可感知的。
> - *onomatopoeia* \[ˌɒnəˌmætəˈpiːə\]：n. 拟声；拟声词。
> - *strong claim*：强断言。

Late in his career, in the chapter "Style and Signification" of *Fiction and Diction*, Genette redefined style with the help of the American philosopher Nelson Goodman's theory of symbols. Goodman points out that a symbol can work in two directions. **Denotation** points to what it stands for: the word "red" refers to red. **Exemplification** displays, and draws attention to, properties **the symbol itself possesses**: a tailor's swatch does not "refer" to anything else; it shows its own color and weave. On this basis Genette argues that **style is the exemplificatory aspect of discourse**: while denoting things, discourse also displays its own perceptible properties, its rhythm, tone, plainness or ornateness, and those properties are its style.

> 热奈特晚年在《虚构与措辞》的“文体与意义”一章中，借用美国哲学家纳尔逊·古德曼的符号理论重新定义了文体。古德曼指出，一个符号可以从两个方向发挥作用。**指谓**：指向它所代表的对象，“红”这个词指向红色。**例示**：展示并让人注意符号**自身具有**的某些性质，裁缝店里的布料小样并不“指向”别的东西，它展示的是自己的颜色和纹理。热奈特据此主张：**文体就是话语的例示层面**：话语在指谓事物的同时，也展示着自身可感的性质，即它的节奏、语气、简繁等，这些性质就是它的文体。

> - *denotation* \[ˌdiːnəʊˈteɪʃn\]：指谓。符号指向其所代表对象的关系。
> - *exemplification* \[ɪɡˌzemplɪfɪˈkeɪʃn\]：例示。符号展示其自身所具有的性质，并以此指涉这些性质。
> - *swatch* \[swɒtʃ\]：n.（布料等的）小样；样品。
> - *ornateness* \[ɔːˈneɪtnəs\]：n. 华丽；繁复。
> - 引文出处：Genette, *Fiction & Diction*, trans. Catherine Porter (Cornell UP, 1993)；Nelson Goodman, *Languages of Art* (1968; 2nd ed. Hackett, 1976)。

This definition has two important corollaries. **First, style is not "deviation from the norm."** Traditional stylistics often understood style as a departure from ordinary usage (the more unusual, the more style); Genette instead holds that all discourse has perceptible properties, so **all discourse has style**. "Plain" does not mean "without style": an unadorned, sustained use of short declarative sentences is itself a describable style. **Second, style is not a wrapper that can be swapped at will.** One cannot first assume that the "content" stays entirely the same and then treat style as an outer shell; tone, rhythm, register, and modes of reference may themselves change the force of a judgment, the attribution of responsibility, and the image of a character. Compare "She finally learned that her father had hidden the letter" with "The letter was hidden by her father. All along." The two convey similar information, but the second **exemplifies**, through pause, repetition, and afterthought, a process of sudden congealing recognition. Analysis should not merely list "short sentences were used," but explain how they work together with the narrative speed, the character's consciousness, and the place of the revelation. Note too that a stretch of language having a certain property does not mean readers must treat it as an aesthetic object; what matters is which properties are noticed and foregrounded in a particular reading.

> 这一定义有两个重要推论。**第一，文体不是“偏离常规”。** 传统文体学常把文体理解为对常规用法的偏离，越反常就越有文体；热奈特则认为任何话语都有可感的性质，因此**一切话语都有文体**。“平淡”不等于“没有文体”：不加修饰、持续使用简短陈述句，本身就是一种可以描述的文体。**第二，文体不是可以随意更换的包装。** 不能先假定“内容”完全不变，再把文体当作外壳；语气、节奏、语体和指称方式本身，就可能改变判断的力度、责任的归属和人物的形象。比较两句话：“她终于知道父亲藏过那封信。”与“信是父亲藏的。一直都是。”两者传达的信息相近，后者却借停顿、重复和补充，**例示**了一种认识骤然凝固的过程。分析时不能只列举“用了短句”，而应说明这些短句如何与前文的叙事速度、人物的意识以及真相揭示的位置共同起作用。还要注意：一段语言具有某种性质，并不意味着读者必然把它当作审美对象；关键要看在具体的阅读中，哪些性质被注意到并得到凸显。

> - *deviation* \[ˌdiːviˈeɪʃn\]：n. 偏离；背离。
> - *stylistics* \[staɪˈlɪstɪks\]：n. 文体学。
> - *unadorned* \[ˌʌnəˈdɔːnd\]：adj. 不加修饰的；朴素的。
> - *wrapper* \[ˈræpə(r)\]：n. 包装；外壳。
> - *congeal* \[kənˈdʒiːl\]：v. 凝固；凝结。
> - *foreground*：v. 凸显；使突出。

## VIII. Fiction and Diction: What Makes a Text Literature 

***Fiction and Diction* (1991) asks on what grounds a text is taken as literature.** The first step is to take apart three propositions that are often run together: "this is fiction," "this belongs to literature," and "this is good literature." None can stand in for another: a badly written novel is still a novel, and still belongs to literature.

> **《虚构与措辞》（1991）要回答的问题是：一个文本凭什么被当作文学？** 第一步是拆开三个常被混为一谈的命题：“这是虚构”、“这属于文学”、“这是优秀的文学”。三者不能互相替代：写得很差的小说仍然是小说，也仍然属于文学。

> - *on what grounds*：凭什么；依据什么理由。
> - *proposition* \[ˌprɒpəˈzɪʃn\]：n. 命题。
> - *run together*：混为一谈。
> - *stand in for*：替代。
> - 引文出处：Genette, *Fiction & Diction*, trans. Catherine Porter (Cornell UP, 1993)；法文原版 *Fiction et diction* (Seuil, 1991)。

Genette offers two pairs of distinctions. The first concerns the **sources** of literariness. **Fiction** means the text constructs imagined characters, events, and situations; the criterion lies in **what it tells**. **Diction** means the linguistic form of the text itself becomes an object of attention and appreciation; the criterion lies in **how it says it**. *Diction* here is not merely "choice of words" but the aesthetically perceptible properties a text has as linguistic form. The second pair concerns the **ways** literary status is established. **Constitutive literariness** means that, under established literary conventions, a text acquires literary status by its type alone: a novel or a poem belongs to literature the moment it is written. **Conditional literariness** means that a text originally serving another purpose is taken as literature because readers read it aesthetically. Crossing the two pairs gives three cases, since fiction is always constitutive and there is no "conditional fiction":

- **Fiction + constitutive**: novels, plays, and other fictional works. Reminder: a bad novel is still a novel.
- **Diction + constitutive**: poetry acquires literary status through its form and literary conventions. Reminder: being a poem does not mean having high value.
- **Diction + conditional**: letters, works of history, essays, and the like, read aesthetically for their expression. Reminder: this literary status changes with modes of reading and historical situation.

> 热奈特给出两组区分。第一组关于文学性的**来源**。**虚构**：文本建构了想象的人物、事件和情境，判断的依据在于它**讲了什么**。**措辞**：文本的语言形式本身成为被注意、被欣赏的对象，判断的依据在于它**怎样说**。这里的 diction 不只是“遣词造句”，而是指文本作为语言形式所具有的、能被审美地感知的性质。第二组关于文学身份成立的**方式**。**构成性文学性**：在既定的文学惯例中，文本仅凭其类型就取得文学身份，一部小说、一首诗，一写出来就属于文学。**条件性文学性**：文本原本有别的用途，因为读者对它进行了审美的阅读，才被当作文学。两组交叉，得到三种情形（虚构总是构成性的，所以不存在“条件性的虚构”）：
>
> - **虚构＋构成性**：小说、戏剧等虚构作品。提醒：写得差的小说仍是小说。
> - **措辞＋构成性**：诗歌凭其形式和文学惯例取得文学身份。提醒：属于诗歌不等于价值很高。
> - **措辞＋条件性**：书信、历史著作、随笔等，因其表达而被审美地阅读。提醒：这种文学身份会随阅读方式和历史处境而变化。

> - *literariness* \[ˈlɪtərərɪnəs\]：n. 文学性。
> - *fiction / diction*：虚构 / 措辞。文学性的两种来源，一在内容的虚构性，一在语言形式的审美性。
> - *constitutive / conditional literariness*：构成性 / 条件性文学性。前者由文类惯例赋予，后者依赖读者的审美阅读。
> - *criterion* \[kraɪˈtɪəriən\]：n. 标准；准则（复数 criteria）。

This is a theoretical distinction about the **sources of literariness and the ways it is recognized**, not a natural definition valid for every culture, and "constitutive" does not mean a given work has the same status under all historical conditions. Back to the case: when the mother wrote her letter, it had a concrete practical purpose, to announce her decision to leave home. Years later the daughter notices its recurring pauses, its restrained forms of address, its sentences that start to speak and fall silent, and begins to treat it as a work of language worth reading again and again. The letter does not thereby lose its identity as a letter, but it gains the possibility of aesthetic reading. **Conditional literariness is not a permanent certificate issued to a nonliterary text; it describes the conditions under which the text is taken as literature.**

> 这是一种关于**文学性的来源及其认定方式**的理论区分，而不是适用于一切文化的自然定义；“构成性”也不意味着某部作品在任何历史条件下都具有同样的身份。回到案例：母亲当年写信，是为了宣布离家的决定，这封信有具体的实际用途。多年后，女儿注意到信中反复出现的停顿、克制的称呼和欲言又止的句子，开始把它当作值得一读再读的语言作品。信并没有因此失去书信的身份，却获得了被审美阅读的可能。**条件性文学性不是给非文学文本颁发的一张永久证书，而是描述它在什么条件下被当作文学。**

> - *certificate* \[səˈtɪfɪkət\]：n. 证书；证明。
> - *forms of address*：称呼方式。

**The difference between fiction and fact cannot be judged by "whether there are realistic details."** "In the summer of 1998 a mother was preparing to leave home" could appear in a novel or in an investigative report. As factual narration, the sentence commits itself to the claim that what it says really happened; as fiction, it invites the reader to understand characters and events within a posited situation. A novel that depicts a real city does not thereby become reportage, and a report that uses flashback, dialogue, and detail does not thereby become a novel. In the chapter "The Fictional Act" Genette draws on ***speech act theory***, which holds that speaking is not only stating facts but also doing things: promising, ordering, declaring are all ways of acting with words. Within this framework the philosopher John Searle regarded the sentences of fiction as "pretended assertions": the author does not really assert that the princess confessed. Genette goes further, treating fictional statements as ***indirect speech acts***: literally they are assertions, but what they actually perform is a different act, **declaring** an imaginary world into being, or inviting the reader to "imagine that...."

> **虚构与真实的区别，不能靠“有没有写实细节”来判断。** “1998年夏天，一位母亲准备离家”既可以出现在小说里，也可以出现在调查报道里。作为事实叙述，这句话承诺所说的事真实发生过；作为虚构，它邀请读者在一个设定的情境中理解人物和事件。小说写了真实的城市，不会因此变成报道；报道用了倒叙、对话和细节，也不会因此变成小说。热奈特在“虚构行为”一章中借助了***言语行为理论***。这一理论认为，说话不只是陈述事实，也是在做事：承诺、命令、宣告都是“以言行事”。在这一框架里，哲学家塞尔认为虚构中的句子是“假装的断言”：作者并不真的断言王妃坦白过。热奈特更进一步，认为虚构陈述是一种***间接言语行为***：字面上是断言，实际完成的却是另一种行为，即**宣告**一个想象世界成立，或者邀请读者“请想象……”。

> - *posited situation*：设定的情境。
> - *reportage* \[ˌrepɔːˈtɑːʒ\]：n. 报道文学；新闻报道。
> - *speech act theory*：言语行为理论。奥斯汀、塞尔等人提出，认为言语本身就是行为，如承诺、命令、宣告。
> - *pretended assertion*：假装的断言。塞尔对虚构句子的定性。
> - *indirect speech act*：间接言语行为。字面上执行一种言语行为，实际上执行另一种，如“你能把盐递给我吗”字面是提问，实际是请求。
> - 引文出处：Jean-Marie Schaeffer, “Fictional vs. Factual Narration,” *LHN*。

It follows that fiction cannot simply be defined as "saying what is false": a lie tries to make someone take an untruth as fact, while ordinary fictional communication never depends on hiding its fictional status. Forged memoirs, fake documents, and hybrid writing do complicate the boundary, and require concrete examination of the commitments made at publication and the strategies the text adopts. In the chapter "Fictional Narrative, Factual Narrative," Genette asks whether the narrative features of Sections I–V can serve as markers of fiction. His answer is a limited yes. Entering several characters' minds without stating sources, for example, is highly compatible with the conventions of fictional narrative; a factual account that did so would have to give its grounds. But "using the third person" or "having internal focalization" is not enough to prove fiction: histories and biographies also reconstruct people's thoughts from letters and interviews; they simply bear the **burden of evidence**.

> 由此可见，虚构不能简单定义为“说假话”：谎言试图让人把不实之辞当作事实，而通常的虚构交流从不以隐藏自身的虚构身份为前提。当然，伪造的回忆录、假文献和混合写作确实会让边界变得复杂，需要具体考察出版时作出的承诺和文本采取的策略。在“虚构叙事与事实叙事”一章中，热奈特追问：第一至五节的那些叙事特征，能否充当虚构的标志？他的回答是有限的肯定。例如，不交代信息来源就进入多个人物的内心，与虚构叙事的惯例高度相容；事实叙述若这样写，就必须说明依据。但“使用第三人称”或“存在内部聚焦”都不足以证明是虚构：历史著作和传记同样会依据书信、访谈重建人物的想法，只是它们要承担**举证责任**。

> - *untruth*：n. 不实之辞；谎言。
> - *hybrid writing*：混合写作，如介于虚构与纪实之间的作品。
> - *marker of fiction*：虚构的标志。
> - *burden of evidence*：举证责任；证据负担。

**The most useful analytic tool is to ask whether author, narrator, and character are the same person.** Writing A for author, N for narrator, and P for the main character:

- **A = N**: factual narrative. The author answers for the truth of what is told.
- **A ≠ N**: fictional narrative. The narrator is a role the author sets up.
- **A = N = P**: autobiography.
- **A ≠ N, N = P**: first-person fiction, such as a fictional autobiographical novel.

In a novel the narrator usually cannot be equated with the real author; an autobiography promises that the two are identical, and the author must take corresponding responsibility for the narration. These relations serve well as **coordinates for analysis**, but should not be detached from complex cases such as anonymity, editing, ghostwriting, and *autofiction* and turned into a formula for classifying every text at a glance.

> **最有用的分析工具，是看作者、叙述者与人物三者是否为同一人。** 以 A 表示作者、N 表示叙述者、P 表示主人公：
>
> - **A＝N**：事实叙述。作者为所述内容的真实性负责。
> - **A≠N**：虚构叙述。叙述者是作者设置的一个角色。
> - **A＝N＝P**：自传。
> - **A≠N，N＝P**：第一人称虚构，例如虚构的自传体小说。
>
> 在小说中，叙述者通常不能与现实作者画等号；自传则承诺两者同一，作者要为叙述承担相应的责任。这组关系适合作为**分析的坐标**，但不能脱离匿名、编辑、代笔、*自我虚构*等复杂情况，变成一眼判定所有文本的公式。

> - *answer for*：对……负责。
> - *coordinates* \[kəʊˈɔːdɪnəts\]：n. 坐标。
> - *ghostwriting*：代笔。
> - *autofiction* \[ˈɔːtəʊˌfɪkʃn\]：自我虚构。作者以真名写入、介于自传与小说之间的写作。
> - *at a glance*：一眼看去；一目了然。

## IX. The Work of Art: Mode of Existence and Aesthetic Relation

**The *ontology of art* asks what exactly we call "the same work."** When a copy of a book is burned, the novel does not vanish, since other copies exist; when a unique painting is destroyed, the loss cannot be made good by printing another. Why the difference? Genette's late two-volume *The Work of Art* (*L'Œuvre de l'art*) treats, in turn, the work's mode of existence (vol. 1, 1994) and our aesthetic reception of works (vol. 2, 1997). The first volume organizes its discussion around the pair ***immanence*** and ***transcendence***. These are technical terms, unrelated to phrases like "inner thought" or "transcendent spirit." Immanence is **what object the work consists of**: a canvas, a text, or something else. Transcendence is how a work **exceeds** a one-to-one correspondence with any single such object.

> **艺术本体论问的是：我们究竟把什么称为“同一部作品”？** 一本书被烧毁，那部小说并不会因此消失，因为还有别的印本；一幅独一无二的画被毁，损失却无法靠重印一张来弥补。为什么会有这种差别？热奈特晚年的两卷本《艺术作品》分别讨论作品的存在方式（第一卷，1994）和我们对作品的审美接受（第二卷，1997）。第一卷用***内在性***与***超越性***这对概念来组织讨论。它们是技术术语，与“内在思想”、“超越精神”之类的说法无关。内在性指**作品由什么对象构成**：一块画布、一份文本，还是别的什么。超越性指作品怎样**超出**它与某一个构成对象之间的一一对应。

> - *ontology of art*：艺术本体论。研究艺术作品是什么、以何种方式存在。
> - *make good*：弥补；赔偿。
> - *immanence (immanence)* \[ˈɪmənəns\]：内在性。作品由哪一种（或哪些）对象构成。
> - *transcendence (transcendance)* \[trænˈsendəns\]：超越性。作品超出其构成对象、不与之一一对应的种种方式。
> - *one-to-one correspondence*：一一对应。
> - 引文出处：Genette, *The Work of Art: Immanence and Transcendence*, trans. G. M. Goshgarian (Cornell UP, 1997)。

Inheriting and reworking a distinction from Nelson Goodman, Genette proposes two **regimes** in which works exist. In the **autographic** regime, the work consists of a physical object (or a batch of them), and its identity depends on **the history of its production**, whether this thing came from that act of making; typical cases are paintings and sculptures, while prints and casts are cases of "one act of production, several originals." In the **allographic** regime, the work consists of an ideal object, such as the structure prescribed by a text or a score, and its identity depends on **sameness of notation**: spell it right, and it is the same work; typical cases are literature and music. This explains the opening question. A painting can be **forged**: a reproduction that looks extremely similar is still not the original, because it did not come from the painter's act of making. Literature has no forgery of the text: any book typeset by someone else carries the same text so long as its words match the original's.

> 热奈特继承并改造了古德曼的一组区分，提出作品存在的两种**体制**。**自笔性体制**：作品由一个（或一批）物理对象构成，其同一性取决于**制作的历史**，即这件东西是否出自那一次创作；典型如绘画、雕塑，版画和铸件则属于“一次制作、多件原作”的情形。**他笔性体制**：作品由一个理想对象构成，例如一份文本或一份乐谱所规定的结构，其同一性取决于**记号是否一致**，拼写对了，就是同一部作品；典型如文学、音乐。这就解释了开头的问题。绘画可以被**伪造**：一幅外观极其相似的复制品，因为不是出自画家那一次创作，仍然不是原作。文学则无所谓文本的伪造：只要字句与原作一致，任何一本由他人排印的书都承载着同一个文本。

> - *regime* \[reɪˈʒiːm\]：n. 体制；制度；方式。
> - *autographic / allographic* \[ˌɔːtəˈɡræfɪk\] / \[ˌæləˈɡræfɪk\]：自笔性的 / 他笔性的。古德曼的区分：前者作品同一性依赖制作史，可被伪造；后者依赖记号系统的拼写一致，不存在伪造。
> - *notation* \[nəʊˈteɪʃn\]：n. 记号；记谱法；标记系统。
> - *forge* \[fɔːdʒ\]：v. 伪造；锻造。
> - *typeset*：v. 排版；排印。

This distinction is **not** a distinction between "only one copy" and "many copies." A print can have several authorized originals and still belong to the autographic regime; a literary work may survive in only one manuscript, but the manuscript's identity as a collector's item is not the identity of the literary text. The real question is: **which properties are constitutive for identifying this work, and which belong only to a particular presentation of it?** In the case: the same words printed in two books with differently colored covers are still the same text; change the ending to "the mother did in fact send the letter," and you have not merely changed the paper but changed the text itself. Yet if a change of typeface alters a poem's visual arrangement, one cannot simply assume the typeface is irrelevant. Which properties belong to the work and which are only incidental features of a presentation must be judged according to the particular work and the norms of its medium.

> 这一区分**并不是**“只有一件”与“可以有很多件”的区别。版画可以有多份经认可的原作，仍属自笔性体制；文学作品也可能只留下一份手稿，但手稿作为收藏品的身份，并不等于文学文本的身份。真正要问的是：**哪些性质对识别这部作品是构成性的，哪些只属于它某一次具体的呈现？** 以案例说明：同一段文字印在两本封面颜色不同的书里，仍是同一个文本；把结尾改成“母亲其实寄出了信”，就不只是换了纸张，而是改变了文本本身。但如果更换字体改变了一首诗的视觉排列，就不能想当然地认为字体无关紧要。哪些性质属于作品本身、哪些只是某次呈现的偶然特征，要根据具体作品及其媒介的规范来判断。

> - *constitutive* \[ˈkɒnstɪtjuːtɪv\]：adj. 构成性的；本质的。
> - *presentation*：n. 呈现；表现形式。
> - *incidental* \[ˌɪnsɪˈdentl\]：adj. 偶然的；附带的。
> - *typeface* \[ˈtaɪpfeɪs\]：n. 字体。

Genette distinguishes three kinds of transcendence. **First, plural objects of immanence**: one work can correspond to several objects that are not entirely identical, such as different versions revised by the author or several replicas of a painting. **Second, partial or indirect manifestation**: we may encounter only fragments, reproductions, or descriptions of a work, having read only excerpts or seen only a photograph of a painting, and still consider that we have encountered "this work." **Third, plural functioning**: the same object of immanence, received in different historical situations, can become different works. Borges's story "Pierre Menard, Author of the *Quixote*" is often used to illustrate this: a twentieth-century man writes, word for word, the same text as Cervantes, yet its meaning is no longer the same.

> 热奈特区分了三种超越性。**第一，多个构成对象**：一部作品可以对应多个不完全相同的对象，例如作者修订的不同版本，或同一幅画的几个复本。**第二，部分或间接的呈现**：我们可能只接触到作品的片段、复制品或描述，只读过节选，或只见过一幅画的照片，却仍然认为自己接触了“这部作品”。**第三，功能的复数化**：同一个构成对象在不同的历史情境中被接受，可以成为不同的作品。博尔赫斯的小说《〈吉诃德〉的作者皮埃尔·梅纳尔》常被用来说明这一点：一个20世纪的人逐字写出与塞万提斯完全相同的文字，意义却已不同。

> - *plural immanence*：多个构成对象；内在性的复数。
> - *partial manifestation*：部分呈现。
> - *plural functioning*：功能的复数化。同一对象在不同语境中作为不同作品发挥作用。
> - *replica* \[ˈreplɪkə\]：n. 复本；复制品。
> - *excerpt* \[ˈeksɜːpt\]：n. 节选；摘录。

**For literary study this means that the text, a particular printed copy, a state of revision, and the work as a whole must each be specified.** When comparing an abridged edition, the first periodical publication, the author's revised edition, and a translation, first say clearly what is being compared: differences in wording, differences in material presentation, or the ways readers group these materials as "the same work." That two printings differ in layout and that two versions alter a character's motive are changes on different levels. Whether an original and its translation count as different presentations of one work or as relatively independent works cannot be settled by saying "the meaning is the same"; and treating a translation's tone as a property of the original language confuses the work's identity with one of its presentations.

> **对文学研究而言，这意味着文本、具体的印本、修订状态和作品整体需要分别说明。** 比较删节本、初刊本、作者修订本和译本时，首先要讲清楚比较的是什么：文字上的差异、物质呈现上的差异，还是读者把这些材料归为“同一部作品”的方式。两个印本排版不同，与两个版本删改了人物动机，是不同层次的变化。原作与译本算同一部作品的不同呈现，还是各自相对独立的作品，不能靠一句“意思一样”来解决；把某个译本的语气当作原作语言本身的性质，也会混淆作品的身份与它的某一次呈现。

> - *abridged edition*：删节本。
> - *periodical publication*：期刊发表；初刊。
> - *layout* \[ˈleɪaʊt\]：n. 版式；排版。
> - *settle*：v. 解决；裁定。

The second volume, *The Aesthetic Relation*, turns to how we attend to and evaluate works. It offers no "scorecard of artistic quality" but distinguishes three moments of reception. **Aesthetic attention** concerns **how an object appears**, its form, organization, and the impression it makes, not only what information it conveys: reading the mother's letter, one asks not only whether she ever bought a train ticket, but notices that she never writes outright "I am leaving." **Aesthetic appreciation** is a judgment of liking or disliking what has been attended to: "The ending is suffocating." The **artistic relation** is awareness that an object was made with some intention, and understanding it accordingly in terms of its genre and historical position: reading the letter as a carefully placed text within an epistolary novel.

> 第二卷《审美关系》转向我们如何注意和评价作品。它不提供一张“艺术质量评分表”，而是区分了接受过程中的三个环节。**审美注意**关注对象**怎样显现**，即它的形态、组织以及给人的感受，而不只是它传达了什么信息：读母亲的信时，不只追问她有没有买过车票，还注意到她始终没有直接写出“我要离开”。**审美评价**是对所注意的对象作出喜欢或不喜欢的判断：“这个结尾令人窒息。”**艺术关系**是意识到对象出于某种意图被制作出来，并据此结合其体裁和历史位置来理解它：把这封信当作一部书信体小说中精心安排的文本来读。

> - *aesthetic attention*：审美注意。关注对象的显现方式本身。
> - *aesthetic appreciation*：审美评价。对所注意对象的喜恶判断。
> - *artistic relation*：艺术关系。把对象当作有意制作的艺术品来理解。
> - *scorecard*：n. 评分表；记分卡。
> - *suffocating* \[ˈsʌfəkeɪtɪŋ\]：adj. 令人窒息的。
> - 引文出处：Genette, *The Aesthetic Relation*, trans. G. M. Goshgarian (Cornell UP, 1999)。

Three points deserve attention. **The object of aesthetic attention need not first be a work of art**: an ordinary letter or a landscape can become one. In literature the properties attended to include sound, rhythm, syntax, meaning, and structure, not only visual appearance, and tracking facts and enjoying expression can go on at once. **"Intention" does not mean the author's authority.** The intention in the artistic relation refers to why we treat an object as something worth understanding artistically; it does not require the reader to know all the author's thoughts, still less grant the author final say over the work's meaning. **Aesthetic judgment depends on the subject, but argument is not useless.** Genette holds a *subjectivist* position: an aesthetic judgment expresses what a particular person feels in a particular situation. When we say "this painting is beautiful," the grammar looks like a statement of an objective property of the painting, and Genette considers this *objectivation* an illusion. That does not make criticism pointless: criticism can draw attention to repetitions, structures, or tones previously overlooked, and correct factual misreadings, thereby changing how a work is experienced; what it cannot do is guarantee that everyone with the same information will have the same feeling.

> 有三点需要注意。**审美注意的对象不必先是艺术品**：一封普通的信、一片风景，都可以成为审美注意的对象。在文学中，被注意的性质包括声音、节奏、句法、意义和结构，而不限于视觉外观；追踪事实与欣赏表达，两种阅读可以同时进行。**“意图”不等于作者的权威。** 艺术关系中的意图，指的是我们为什么把一个对象当作值得以艺术方式来理解的东西，并不要求读者知道作者的全部想法，更不意味着作者对作品的意义拥有最终的裁决权。**审美判断依赖主体，但论证并非无用。** 热奈特持*主观主义*立场：审美判断表达的是某个人在某种处境中的感受。我们说“这幅画很美”，语法上像是在陈述画的一种客观属性，热奈特认为这种*客观化*其实是一种错觉。但这并不意味着批评毫无意义：批评可以让人注意到此前忽略的重复、结构或语气，可以纠正事实性的误读，从而改变作品被体验的方式；它只是不能保证所有人在得到同样的信息后产生同样的感受。

> - *final say*：最终决定权。
> - *subjectivist* \[səbˈdʒektɪvɪst\]：adj. 主观主义的。认为审美判断最终依赖于主体感受的立场。
> - *objectivation*：客观化。把主观感受表述为对象的客观属性。
> - *overlook* \[ˌəʊvəˈlʊk\]：v. 忽略；俯瞰。

For example, "the ending is suffocating" is an appreciation; "the last five sentences withdraw, one after another, every possible action the character could take, leaving only the letter that cannot be sent" supplies formal grounds that can be checked, so that the appreciation can be discussed. Another reader may accept the structure and still find it overdesigned. The disagreement need not come down to one side failing to understand; equally, the subjectivity of aesthetic judgment does not exempt false statements about the text from scrutiny.

> 例如，“这个结尾令人窒息”是一个评价；“最后五句话接连撤去人物一切可能的行动，只剩下那封无法寄出的信”则提供了可以检查的形式依据，使评价能够被讨论。另一位读者可以承认这一结构，却觉得它设计得过了头。双方的分歧不必归结为某一方没读懂；同样，审美依赖主体，也不能让关于文本的错误陈述免于检验。

> - *overdesigned*：adj. 设计过度的；过于刻意的。
> - *come down to*：归结为。
> - *exempt ... from*：使……免于。
> - *scrutiny* \[ˈskruːtəni\]：n. 检验；仔细审查。

## X. A Worked Analysis

**The value of a theory lies in whether its parts can work together in one complete reading.** Before the demonstration, one discipline running through this whole guide should be made explicit: a complete argument has at least three layers, and each must be supported by the one beneath it. A **formal judgment** describes what the text does: "the work uses an analepsis here." A **mechanism judgment** explains what effect that form produces: "the analepsis means that the mother's early action is explained only after the daughter has grown up." A **meaning interpretation** explains what that effect signifies: "this arrangement shows how family memory obscures women's experience." The third layer must rest on the first two and on concrete textual evidence; a single term cannot prove a theme. This is also the division of labor between **poetics** and **interpretation**: interpretation asks "what does this letter symbolize?", poetics asks "how can a letter, as an embedded text, organize time, restrict information, and redistribute knowledge among characters?" Poetics supplies interpretation with tools, but the tools do not automatically yield a single answer.

> **理论的价值，在于它的各部分能否在一次完整的阅读中协同工作。** 在示范之前，先明确一条贯穿全文的分析纪律：一个完整的论证至少包含三个层次，每一层都必须由下一层支撑。**形式判断**描述文本做了什么：“作品在此处采用了回叙。”**机制判断**说明这种形式产生了什么效果：“回叙使母亲早年的行动直到女儿成年后才得到解释。”**意义解释**说明这种效果意味着什么：“这一安排表现了家庭记忆对女性经验的遮蔽。”第三层必须以前两层和具体的文本证据为依据，单凭一个术语不能证明一个主题。这也是**诗学**与**阐释**的分工：阐释问“这封信象征什么”，诗学问“书信作为嵌入文本，可以怎样组织时间、限制信息、重新分配人物之间的知识”。诗学为阐释提供工具，但工具本身不会自动给出唯一的答案。

> - *formal judgment / mechanism judgment / meaning interpretation*：形式判断 / 机制判断 / 意义解释。论证的三个层次，后一层必须以前一层为依据。
> - *obscure* \[əbˈskjʊə(r)\]：v. 遮蔽；使模糊。
> - *division of labor*：分工。
> - *poetics* \[pəʊˈetɪks\]：诗学。研究文学作品得以成立的一般规则与手段。
> - *interpretation*：阐释。解释具体作品的意义。

The following passage is again of our own invention, used to show how an argument is built step by step from evidence:

*Brother, even today, in 2025, I dare not say I have finally understood our mother. It was the night our father died that I first opened that drawer. For twenty-two months after, I spread the letter on my desk every day, and not once did I show it to you.*

*Do you remember the summer of 1998? Whenever it rained, our mother sat by the door until the water had finished dripping from the eaves. Back then I only thought she felt the cold. Not until I read the letter in 2020 did I learn that she had meant to leave that summer.*

*I opened the letter. She was going to leave? Leave us here? There was only one line on the paper that I read over and over: "Tomorrow I will leave. As for why I stayed so many years, I still can't say."*

*I folded the letter back the way it had been. I did not know then that two years later you would say the one who had really hidden this was me.*

> 以下片段仍为自拟，用来示范如何从证据一步步形成论点：
>
> 弟弟，直到2025年的今天，我仍不敢说自己终于懂了母亲。父亲去世那晚，我才打开那只抽屉。此后二十二个月，我每天都把信摊在桌上，却一次也没有给你看。
>
> 你还记得1998年的夏天吗？母亲每逢下雨就坐在门边，等水从屋檐落尽。我当时只觉得她怕冷。直到2020年读到信，我才知道她曾准备在那个夏天离开。
>
> 我拆开信。她竟然要走？把我们留在这里？纸上只有一行字让我反复看了许久：“明天我会离开。至于为什么留下过这么多年，我现在还说不清。”
>
> 我把信折回原样。那时我并不知道，两年后你会说，真正隐瞒了这件事的人是我。

> - *eaves* \[iːvz\]：n. 屋檐。
> - *build an argument*：形成论点；构建论证。

**Step one: establish the situation of telling (voice).** As to the time of narrating, the opening places the telling in 2025, and "the night our father died" corresponds to 2020; the telling comes after the events, so this is **subsequent narrating**. As to the narratee, Lin Lan is explicitly speaking to her brother, who is a named narratee; since her telling stands at the extradiegetic level, the brother as narratee also stands at the extradiegetic level, even though he is himself a character in the story, while the real reader occupies an overhearing position. As to level and participation, if this is the opening of the novel with no further outer frame, Lin Lan's telling stands at the **extradiegetic** level; she took part in opening and concealing the letter, so the narration is **homodiegetic**. Whether to call it further **autodiegetic** depends on whether the main action of the whole work centers on the daughter or on the mother as told through the daughter, and cannot be decided by the first person alone.

> **第一步：确定讲述情境（语态）。** 叙述时间：开头点明讲述发生在2025年，“父亲去世那晚”对应2020年；讲述在事件之后，是**事后叙述**。受述者：林岚明确在对弟弟说话，弟弟是被点名的受述者；由于林岚的讲述位于故事外层，作为受述者的弟弟也处于故事外层，尽管他本人是故事中的人物；现实读者则处于旁听的位置。叙述层与参与关系：如果这就是小说的开头，没有更外层的框架，林岚的讲述位于**故事外层**；她参与了拆信和隐瞒，是**同故事叙述**。是否进一步称为**自身故事叙述**，要看全篇的主要行动是围绕女儿，还是围绕经由女儿讲出的母亲，不能仅凭第一人称决定。

> - *situation of telling*：讲述情境。
> - *outer frame*：外层框架。

**Step two: sort out the order of events and of text (order).** The passage is told in this sequence: the reflection of 2025 → the discovery of 2020 → the twenty-two months of rereading afterward → the past of 1998 → the scene of opening the letter in 2020 → the quarrel of 2022. Within a single subsequent narration, analepsis and prolepsis can thus alternate. The last sentence, "two years later you would say...", is a prolepsis. The mother's "Tomorrow I will leave," by contrast, is first of all only her plan or announcement at the time; the passage itself does not prove that she actually left, so it cannot be treated as a future event that came true.

> **第二步：整理事件与文本的顺序（顺序）。** 片段的讲述顺序是：2025年的感慨 → 2020年的发现 → 此后二十二个月反复读信 → 1998年的往事 → 2020年拆信的场景 → 2022年的争执。可见在同一段事后叙述中，回叙与预叙可以交替出现。最后一句“两年后你会说……”是预叙。而母亲信中的“明天我会离开”，首先只是她当时的计划或宣告；片段本身并没有证明她后来真的离开了，所以不能把它当作已经实现的未来事件。

> - *sort out*：整理；理清。
> - *come true*：实现；成真。

**Step three: observe duration and frequency.** "For twenty-two months after, I spread the letter on my desk every day" is **iterative** (many readings told once) and at the same time compresses a long stretch by **summary**; after "I opened the letter," the text turns to a more fully developed **scene**. The repeated reading makes "discovering the old letter" no longer a one-time shock but a **sustained concealment**. "And not once did I show it to you" then ties this daily habit to the brother's position as narratee: the present telling is making up for a communication long deferred.

> **第三步：观察时距与频率。** “此后二十二个月，我每天都把信摊在桌上”是**迭述**（许多次阅读只讲一次），同时以**概述**压缩了一段较长的时间；“我拆开信”之后则转为一个较充分展开的**场景**。反复阅读使“发现旧信”不再只是一次性的震惊，而变成一种**持续的隐瞒**。“却一次也没有给你看”又把这个日常习惯与弟弟的受述位置联系起来：眼前的这次讲述，正是在弥补一次拖延已久的交流。

> - *sustained concealment*：持续的隐瞒。
> - *deferred* \[dɪˈfɜːd\]：adj. 推迟的；延期的。

**Step four: analyze focalization and the presentation of speech (mode).** On the two selves: "Back then I only thought she felt the cold" keeps the past misunderstanding, while "not until 2020 did I learn" reinterprets the past with later knowledge; the tension between the narrating I and the experiencing I appears here. On the presentation of consciousness: "She was going to leave? Leave us here?" directly presents emotional inner speech of the time. It is better described as **free direct** presentation of consciousness: nothing here converts a first-person thought into the syntax of third-person narration, so the absence of "I thought" does not make it free indirect style. On the choice of quotation: the mother's letter appears in direct speech, as if she "spoke for herself"; but the quotation was selected by the daughter, and "only one line... that I read over and over" reminds us that she has not given the whole letter. That is not enough to conclude she is deliberately distorting it, but enough to raise questions: **why choose only this line? What image of the mother does it shape? Were reasons left out?** Moreover, this sentence is mainly a decision and a brief self-explanation rather than a relatively independent narration of the past, so the mere quoting of the letter does not yet establish a metadiegetic level.

> **第四步：分析聚焦与话语呈现（语式）。** 两个自我：“我当时只觉得她怕冷”保留了过去的误解，“直到2020年……才知道”则以后来的认识重新解释过去，叙述之我与经历之我的张力在这里显现出来。意识呈现：“她竟然要走？把我们留在这里？”直接呈现了当时情绪化的内心语言。它更适合描述为**自由直接**的意识呈现：这里并没有把第一人称的想法转入第三人称句法的结构，不能因为没有“我想”就判为自由间接话语。引语的选择：母亲的信以直接引语出现，仿佛让她“亲自发言”；但引文经过女儿的挑选，“只有一行字让我反复看了许久”提醒我们，她没有提供信的全文。这还不足以断定她蓄意歪曲，却足以引出问题：**她为什么只选这一行？这一行塑造了怎样的母亲形象？有没有被略去的理由？** 此外，这句话主要是一个决定和一段简短的自我说明，并没有展开一段相对独立的往事叙述，所以仅凭信被引用，还不能说这里构成了元故事层。

> - *presentation of consciousness*：意识呈现。
> - *distort* \[dɪˈstɔːt\]：v. 歪曲；扭曲。
> - *establish a level*：构成（独立的）叙述层。

**Step five: form a thesis.** "Through the adult narrator's retrospection on past experience, the iterative account of years of rereading, and an explanation explicitly addressed to the brother, the passage gradually turns 'discovering the truth' into 'examining one's own concealment.' The mother is first suspected of 'abandoning her children'; the daughter then turns out to have taken up the position of controlling information herself." **This interpretation arises from the combined action of time, frequency, the narratee relation, and the choice of quotation**, not from the fixed meaning of any single term.

> **第五步：形成论点。** “片段通过成年叙述者对过去经验的回溯、对长年读信的迭述，以及明确面向弟弟的解释，把‘发现真相’逐步转化为‘审视自身的隐瞒’。母亲起初被怀疑‘要抛下孩子’，女儿随后却也进入了控制信息的位置。”**这一解释来自时间、频率、受述关系与引语选择的共同作用**，而不是来自任何一个术语的固定含义。

> - *thesis* \[ˈθiːsɪs\]：n. 论点；论题。
> - *retrospection* \[ˌretrəˈspekʃn\]：n. 回溯；回顾。

**Step six: test the limits and alternative explanations.** On the limits of evidence: the early appearance of the brother's accusation at the end does not prove the accusation just; that the daughter concealed something does not automatically prove she shared her father's motive. If the full text revealed that the brother was gravely ill at the time, or that the mother had asked for secrecy, the ethical assessment would change. A good interpretation states what evidence it depends on, and also **what new evidence would force it to be revised**. On a paratextual hypothesis: give the passage the title *Mother's Secret*, and the title first assigns the secret to the mother, while the text gradually makes father and daughter both concealers; reading thereby corrects the initial attribution of blame. On the choice of tools: with no existing hypotext, one cannot claim a hypertextual relation merely from family subject matter; with no differing versions, there is no need to invoke the study of a work's identity. **Mastering the theory systematically means selecting the tools relevant to the problem, not using every concept in every analysis.**

> **第六步：检验边界与替代解释。** 证据的限度：结尾提前出现了弟弟的指责，但这并不证明这一指责必然公正；女儿有所隐瞒，也不自动证明她与父亲出于相同的动机。如果全文交代弟弟当时病重，或母亲曾要求保密，伦理评价就会改变。好的解释要说明自己依赖哪些证据，也要说明**什么样的新证据会促使它被修正**。副文本假设：如果给片段加上《母亲的秘密》这个标题，标题会先把秘密归到母亲身上，正文却逐渐使父女都成为隐瞒者，阅读由此修正了最初的归责。工具的取舍：没有现成的底本，就不能仅凭家庭题材硬说存在超文本关系；没有不同的版本，也不必套用作品同一性的研究。**系统掌握理论，意味着挑选与问题相关的工具，而不是在每篇分析中用遍全部概念。**

> - *alternative explanation*：替代解释。
> - *attribution of blame*：归责。
> - *invoke* \[ɪnˈvəʊk\]：v. 援用；调用。

## XI. Transferring the Method, Limits of the Theory, and a Learning Path

The "theoretical system" described in this guide is **a learning framework reconstructed from Genette's main works**, not a closed system he himself proposed. He never wrote a comprehensive treatise, and his concepts shifted across periods (as *Narrative Discourse Revisited* revised *Narrative Discourse*). Looking back, the framework comprises five fields of inquiry:

- **Theory of narrative discourse** (Sections I–V): the relations among story, narrative, and narrating. Core question: why can the same events yield different narrative experiences?
- **Transtextuality and genre theory** (Section VI): the relations of a text to other texts, to genres, and to its surrounding materials. Core question: how does a work gain meaning through existing literary and publishing arrangements?
- **Rhetoric, style, and mimology** (Section VII): linguistic form and the properties by which it is perceived. Core question: why does an expression seem terse, solemn, or as if fitted to the thing itself?
- **Theory of fiction and literariness** (Section VIII): the communicative and literary status of a text. Core question: what makes a text read as fiction, and read as literature?
- **Ontology of art and aesthetics** (Section IX): the work, its particular presentations, and our reception. Core question: how does one work exist in several versions, and how does it become an aesthetic object?

> 本文所说的热奈特“理论体系”，是**根据他的主要著作重建出来的学习框架**，并不是他本人提出的封闭体系。他没有写过一部总论，各个时期的概念也有调整（例如《新叙事话语》对《叙事话语》的修订）。回顾前文，这个框架包含五个研究领域：
>
> - **叙事话语理论**（第一至五节）：处理故事、叙事与叙述之间的关系。核心问题：相同的事件为什么能形成不同的叙事经验？
> - **跨文本关系与体裁理论**（第六节）：处理一个文本与其他文本、体裁及周边材料之间的关系。核心问题：一部作品怎样借助既有的文学与出版安排获得意义？
> - **修辞、文体与语言拟仿**（第七节）：处理语言形式及其被感知的性质。核心问题：某种表达为什么显得简洁、庄重，或仿佛贴合事物本身？
> - **虚构与文学性理论**（第八节）：处理文本的交际身份与文学身份。核心问题：什么使一个文本被当作虚构来读、被当作文学来读？
> - **艺术本体论与审美理论**（第九节）：处理作品、它的具体呈现以及我们的接受活动。核心问题：同一部作品怎样存在于多个版本中，又怎样成为审美对象？

> - *reconstruct* \[ˌriːkənˈstrʌkt\]：v. 重建；重构。
> - *treatise* \[ˈtriːtɪs\]：n. 专著；论著。
> - *terse* \[tɜːs\]：adj. 简洁的；精炼的。

**These fields run into one another; they are not sealed-off districts.** Free indirect style is at once a mode of speech presentation (Section III) and a question of style (Section VII); the word "memoir" on a cover is both a paratext (Section VI) and a factor in judging fictionality (Section VIII); a rewriting can be studied as a hypertext (Section VI) and also raises questions of the work's identity (Section IX). To carry the method over to other novels, **begin from a concrete reading difficulty and let the problem choose the tools.** "Why do I know this character's thoughts so well yet trust his explanations less and less?" may call for analyzing internal focalization, free indirect style, and unreliable judgment together. "Why does the mother's experience take up only a few pages yet determine the course of the whole book?" may call for analyzing ellipsis, completing analepsis, and the mismatch between space and causal weight. **Using more terms does not mean analyzing more deeply.**

> **这些领域彼此贯通，不是互不往来的分区。** 自由间接话语既是话语呈现方式（第三节），也是文体问题（第七节）；封面上的“回忆录”字样既是副文本（第六节），又影响对虚构性的判断（第八节）；一个改写本既可以作为超文本来研究（第六节），也会引出作品同一性的问题（第九节）。要把这套方法用到其他小说上，**最好从一个具体的阅读困难开始，让问题来决定用哪些工具。** “为什么我很了解这个人物的想法，却越来越不信任他的解释？”可能需要同时分析内部聚焦、自由间接话语和不可靠的判断。“为什么母亲的经历只占了几页，却决定了全书的走向？”可能需要分析省略、补充性回叙，以及篇幅与因果分量之间的错位。**术语用得多，不等于分析得深。**

> - *run into one another*：彼此交融；相互贯通。
> - *sealed-off*：adj. 封闭隔绝的。
> - *carry over*：迁移；沿用到。
> - *causal weight*：因果分量，即某一事件在因果链条中的重要程度。

A reusable workflow:

1. **Fix the material and scope.** Record the edition, translation, chapters, and specific passages used, and keep the sources of quotations.
2. **Reconstruct only the story relations you need.** Sort out events relevant to the question; mark separately the established facts, the characters' claims, and what remains undecided.
3. **Identify the formal arrangements.** Point to observable evidence in order, duration, frequency, voice, or focalization.
4. **Explain the local mechanism.** Show how these arrangements distribute information, time, opportunities to speak, or the authority to judge.
5. **Connect to larger meaning.** Check whether the mechanism recurs, changes, or is contradicted in other passages, and only then discuss theme and value.
6. **Test alternative explanations.** Imagine how a different way of writing would change things, and check whether your thesis has mistaken "related" for "necessary."

> 一套可以反复使用的工作流程：
>
> 1. **确定材料和范围。** 记下所用的版本、译本、章节和具体段落，保留引文的出处。
> 2. **只重建必要的故事关系。** 整理与问题有关的事件；把确定的事实、人物的说法和尚无定论之处分别标出。
> 3. **找出形式安排。** 指出顺序、时距、频率、语态或聚焦方面可以观察到的证据。
> 4. **解释局部机制。** 说明这些安排怎样分配信息、时间、发言机会或判断的权威。
> 5. **联系更大的意义。** 检查这一机制是否在其他段落重复出现、发生变化或受到反驳，然后再讨论主题与价值。
> 6. **检验替代解释。** 设想换一种写法会改变什么，检查自己的论点是否把“相关”误当成了“必然”。

> - *workflow*：n. 工作流程。
> - *undecided*：adj. 尚无定论的。
> - *mistake ... for ...*：把……误当成……

**The rewriting experiment is especially effective.** Rewrite a passage of internal focalization as zero focalization: if a character's misunderstanding is then exposed at once, you can see what the original restriction of information was doing. Rewrite the iterative account of repeatedly opening the letter as three distinct scenes, and you can compare how "a pattern of life" and "a series of changes" are each produced. The aim is not to write a worse version to prove the original great, but to make the consequences of formal choices easier to see. When writing up an analysis, try completing this sentence: "**Through the formal arrangement of \_\_\_, this passage makes \_\_\_ information or experience appear in \_\_\_ way, and thereby changes \_\_\_ here; this interpretation is supported by \_\_\_, but must still take account of \_\_\_.**" The hardest and most instructive part is to make clear the relation on either side of "thereby." **Once that relation can be checked in the text, questioned by other readers, and revised in light of evidence, the terminology has become a research skill.**

> **改写实验尤其有效。** 把一段内部聚焦改写为零聚焦，如果人物的误解随即被提前揭穿，就能看出原来的信息限制起了什么作用；把反复拆信的迭述改写为三次各不相同的场景，就能比较“一种生活模式”与“一连串变化”分别是如何形成的。实验的目的不是写一个更差的版本来证明原作伟大，而是让形式选择的后果更容易被看清。撰写分析时，可以试着补全这个句式：“**这段文本通过＿＿的形式安排，使＿＿信息或经验以＿＿方式出现，因此在这里改变了＿＿；这一解释由＿＿支持，但仍需考虑＿＿。**”其中最难、也最有学习价值的，是把“因此”前后的关系讲清楚。**只要这一关系能够在文本中被核对、被其他读者质疑、并依据证据得到修正，术语就已经转化成了研究能力。**

> - *rewriting experiment*：改写实验。
> - *instructive* \[ɪnˈstrʌktɪv\]：adj. 有启发的；有教益的。
> - *in light of*：根据；鉴于。

**Four limits of the theory should be kept in view.** **Descriptive precision is not evaluative judgment**: a narrator is not automatically reliable for using the third person; a focal character is not necessarily endorsed by the work because he occupies many pages; a work is not necessarily complex or excellent because it mixes many devices. Genettean analysis first supplies descriptive precision, and literary value must be argued separately. **A change of medium requires retesting**: the model grew mainly from literary narrative, especially the novel, and when used on film, games, theater, or oral storytelling one must test what work camera, performance, sound, and interaction are doing, rather than transfer the categories of the printed text unchanged. **Formal analysis needs to be joined with other research**: how gendered power forms, why a given behavior was sanctioned under particular historical conditions, and what emotions actual readers felt cannot be fully answered by formal classification alone; one can begin by discovering from the distribution of information who has long been unable to speak directly, then interpret its significance with historical and social research. **The theory itself keeps developing**: later narratology has proposed revisions on focalization (Bal), on whether a narrator must always exist, on *unnatural narrative*, and more. Genette should be learned as a theory open to discussion and correction, not as a set of rules forbidding works to cross boundaries.

> **要时刻记住这套理论的四个限度。** **描述的精确不等于价值的判断**：叙述者不会因为用了第三人称就自动可靠；聚焦人物不会因为占了大量篇幅就必然得到作品的认同；作品也不会因为混用了多种手法就必然复杂或优秀。热奈特式的分析首先提供描述的精度，文学价值仍需另行论证。**换一种媒介需要重新检验**：这套模型主要从文学叙事、尤其是小说中发展出来，用它分析电影、游戏、戏剧或口头讲述时，要检验镜头、表演、声音和互动分别承担了什么功能，而不能把印刷文本的范畴原样照搬。**形式分析需要与其他研究结合**：性别权力如何形成、某种行为在特定历史条件下为何被认可、读者实际产生了何种情绪，都无法仅靠形式分类得到完整的答案；可以先从信息的分配中发现谁长期无法直接发言，再结合历史与社会研究解释其意义。**理论本身仍在发展**：后来的叙事学在聚焦（巴尔）、叙述者是否必然存在、*非自然叙事*等问题上都提出了修订。应当把热奈特当作一套可以讨论、可以校正的理论来学习，而不是一套禁止作品越界的规则。

> - *descriptive precision*：描述的精确。
> - *evaluative judgment*：价值判断。
> - *focal character*：聚焦人物。
> - *sanction* \[ˈsæŋkʃn\]：v. 认可；准许；n. 制裁。此处取“认可”义。
> - *unnatural narrative*：非自然叙事。研究违反现实世界逻辑的叙事（如不可能的叙述者、倒流的时间）的叙事学分支。

**Self-test.** Decide for yourself first, then check the answers that follow.

1. A third-person novel follows a different character strictly in each chapter. Since it enters several characters' minds, is it zero focalization?
2. Recalling events of 2020 from 2025, the narrator mentions in advance a quarrel of 2022. Is this prior narrating, or a prolepsis within subsequent narrating?
3. The protagonist recalls her childhood but does not take up an independent act of telling. Does this necessarily add a narrative level?
4. The same quarrel is told once by each of three people. How should it be described in terms of frequency, and in terms of focalization?
5. A character's words are quoted at length in direct speech, while the context keeps exposing his prejudice. Does direct quotation mean the work endorses him?
6. An interview with the author is later included as an appendix in a new edition. What has changed in its paratextual type?
7. Printing a novel on different paper and rewriting its ending: do the two affect the work's identity in the same way?

> **自测题。** 先自己判断，再对照后面的答案。
>
> 1. 一部第三人称小说，每一章都严格跟随一个不同的人物。既然它进入了多个人物的内心，是否就是零聚焦？
> 2. 2025年回忆2020年的事时，提前讲到了2022年的一次争吵。这是事前叙述，还是事后叙述中的预叙？
> 3. 主人公回想童年，但并没有承担一段独立的讲述。这是否必然增加了一个叙述层？
> 4. 同一次争吵由三个人各讲了一遍。从频率和聚焦两个角度，分别应该怎样描述？
> 5. 一个人物的话被大段直接引用，上下文却不断暴露他的偏见。直接引用是否意味着作品认同他？
> 6. 一篇作者访谈后来被收进新版附录，它的副文本类型发生了什么变化？
> 7. 换一种纸张印刷与改写结尾，对作品同一性的影响一样吗？

> - *self-test*：自测。
> - *appendix* \[əˈpendɪks\]：n. 附录。

**Answers.**

1. Not necessarily. First consider **variable internal focalization**, then check for information that breaks each chapter's restriction (paralepsis).
2. **A prolepsis within subsequent narrating.** For the teller in 2025 both events have already happened; only relative to the 2020 the text is currently telling is 2022 still in the future.
3. Not necessarily. Recollection first concerns time and consciousness; only when the structure sets up a new act of telling does the question of level arise.
4. In frequency it is **repetitive narration** (one event, three tellings); if each telling is presented according to its teller's own experience, in focalization it is **multiple internal focalization**.
5. No. The frame around the quotation can still produce irony.
6. **An epitext has become a peritext**; its readership and the durability of its preservation may change with it.
7. No. Changing the paper alters only a particular presentation; rewriting the ending alters the constitution of the text and usually touches the work's identity.

> **参考答案。**
>
> 1. 不一定。应先考虑**变换内部聚焦**，再检查是否有突破各章限制的信息（即信息增叙）。
> 2. **事后叙述中的预叙。** 对2025年的讲述者而言，两件事都已发生；只是相对于文本正在讲的2020年，2022年还在未来。
> 3. 不必然。回忆首先涉及时间和意识；只有当具体结构建立起一个新的讲述行为时，才需要讨论层次问题。
> 4. 频率上是**重复叙述**（一次事件、三次讲述）；如果每次都按讲述者自己的经验来呈现，聚焦上就构成**多重内部聚焦**。
> 5. 不意味着。引文周围的框架仍然可以产生反讽。
> 6. **外副文本变成了内副文本**；它的读者范围和保存的持久性也可能随之改变。
> 7. 不一样。换纸张只改变了某一次具体的呈现；改结局则改变了文本的构成，通常也触及作品的身份。

> - *durability* \[ˌdjʊərəˈbɪləti\]：n. 持久性；耐久性。
> - *constitution* \[ˌkɒnstɪˈtjuːʃn\]：n. 构成；组成方式；宪法。此处取“构成”义。

**A reading path.** Arrange your reading by question, and bring a short passage of fiction to test against each book, so that theoretical definitions and textual judgments keep moving back and forth.

1. **How do time, mode, and voice in narrative work together?** Read *Narrative Discourse*.
2. **What criticisms did these concepts meet, and how were they revised?** Read *Narrative Discourse Revisited*.
3. **How does a work rewrite earlier works and belong to a genre?** Read *Palimpsests* and *The Architext*.
4. **How do the "thresholds" of publication and reception shape reading?** Read *Paratexts* (*Seuils*).
5. **Why can language become an aesthetic object, and what is fiction?** Read *Fiction and Diction* and *Mimologics*.
6. **In what way do works exist, and how are they received aesthetically?** Read the two volumes of *The Work of Art*.

Chinese readers may consult 王文融's translation 《叙事话语 新叙事话语》 (中国社会科学出版社, 1990), checking terms against the English translation. Chinese titles of Genette's books vary, so it is best to give the original title as well when citing.

> **阅读路线。** 可以按问题来安排阅读顺序，每读一部，都带上一小段小说作检验材料，让理论定义与文本判断不断往返。
>
> 1. **叙事的时间、语式与语态如何协同运作？** 读《叙事话语》。
> 2. **这些概念受到了哪些批评，又怎样被修订？** 读《新叙事话语》。
> 3. **作品怎样改写前作、归属于体裁？** 读《隐迹稿本》和《类属文本导论》。
> 4. **出版与接受的“门槛”怎样塑造阅读？** 读《门槛》。
> 5. **语言为何能成为审美对象？什么是虚构？** 读《虚构与措辞》和《拟仿论》。
> 6. **作品以什么方式存在，又如何被审美地接受？** 读《艺术作品》两卷。
>
> 中文读者可参考王文融译《叙事话语 新叙事话语》（中国社会科学出版社，1990），并对照英译本核对术语。热奈特各书的中译名并不统一，引用时最好同时注明原文书名。

> - *reading path*：阅读路线。
> - *move back and forth*：来回往返。
